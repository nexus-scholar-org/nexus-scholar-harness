**Purpose.** Tell the engine which papers of the first sample do not belong to the question, so the directions, counts, synonym suggestions and seeds are drawn from the papers that do, and see at once whether the sample is still enough to go on.

**Why.** In the real run of 2026-11-13 the 25-paper sample was dominated by off-topic papers and the three proposed directions were generic. The researcher could see the papers but had no way to say "this paper does not belong", so every number on the later screens kept counting it. Nothing on this screen says which papers are off topic; the researcher decides, and the engine only stops counting them.

**Changed from the canvas and from `02-explore`.**
- **A per-paper control, new.** `02` lists papers with a DOI link and an anchors count and nothing else. Each row gains a text button "Not relevant" and, once pressed, an undo (see States). The canvas has no such control.
- **The excluded paper is not deleted from the pool.** The pool file is content-addressed and shared: `probe_pool` caches it by query key (`recon/engine.py:356-357`), so two drafts with the same query read the same file, and `pool_fingerprint` (`session.py:86`) is what a bound direction is checked against (`grounding.check_binding`). Writing exclusions into the pool would change its fingerprint, leak one draft's judgement into another and make "undo" a rewrite of a cache file. Exclusions are stored with the draft instead (see Data out) and applied when the pool is read.
- **"Searching again" does not refill the sample.** Stated plainly in "Refill" below: today a refresh returns the same 25 papers.
- **The thin-pool tile keeps its floor.** `gates.pool` (`POOL_THIN_FLOOR` 12) is computed from what is left after exclusions; this spec adds a recommendation on top, not a second floor for the same tile.
- **Machine output stays a draft.** Directions, schools, topics and synonym suggestions keep the draft styling of `01`; only the researcher's exclusions are written as the researcher's choices (a plain card, solid `hairline` border, no `-soft` fill behind ink text).

## Entry / exit

- Exit: unchanged from `02` ("See {n} directions", "Change scope", "Save draft and exit"). Marking a paper is saved at once; there is no separate Save and leaving needs no dialog.

## What an excluded paper stops feeding
Read from the code; the pool the engine uses after this milestone is the **effective pool** (docs minus exclusions, plus refill, see below), and every row of this table is a place that today reads the raw pool and must read the effective one (✗ M10b, one function `effective_pool(pool, exclusions)` called where `latest_pool` is called now).

| Output | How it is computed from the pool today | What changes when a paper is excluded |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Direction proposals (`03`, and the "See {n} directions" count on `02`) | `directions_for` (`session.py:504`): `_grounded_directions_for_terms` over `micro_taxonomy`, which `_evidence_docs` builds only from docs that **have a DOI and some title or abstract text** (`distiller.py:67-87`); a term needs at least 2 distinct anchor DOIs, then `term_evidence` must again find it in at least 2 records; at most 3 are kept after the family filter | The paper's DOI leaves every term's `anchor_dois`; a term that falls under 2 anchors stops being a direction; the order (longer phrase, then `freq`) can change. This is what removes off-topic phrases from the proposals. |
| Per-term counts and "in {anchored_in} of {n_docs}" (`02` concepts card, `05`) | `concept_report` over `_docs(pool)` (DOI docs only, `vocabulary.py:29`); `n_docs` is `len(pool.docs)` (`recon.py` `concepts`) | The paper no longer counts for any term; `n_docs`, the denominator, is the effective count (so a term in 4 of 25 becomes 4 of 24 if the paper did not contain it). |
| The evidence check for the researcher's own phrase (`GET .../recon/evidence`) | `term_evidence(pool, term)`: whole-word occurrence in DOI docs | Same: excluded papers do not count; `n_docs` is the effective count. |
| Synonym suggestions (`05`, "Terms from your first search") | `concept_report`: terms (1 to 3 words) in at least `SUGGESTION_MIN_RECORDS` 2 of the records that anchor the concept, at most 10 | They are counted only over the remaining records, so words that appeared only because of an off-topic paper drop out. A suggestion the researcher already ticked stays in the draft (they ticked it; nothing is removed silently). |
| Eligibility as a seed (`03` seeds card, `GET .../recon/seeds`) | `seed_candidates`: any DOI doc that anchors a direction or a concept | An excluded paper is never offered. If it is already in `draft.golden_seeds`, see "Marking a saved seed" under States. |
| Schools, topics, metrics, datasets, `qei`, `gates.purity` (`02`) | `distill_pool` over the DOI docs (`_schools`, `_topic_layer`, `_keyword_counts`, `_echo_index`) | Recomputed over the remaining papers; `top3_share` can move across 0.50 (`fragmented` to `coherent`, or back). |
| The size tile and the floor (`gates.pool`, 12) | `compute_pool_sufficiency(len(docs))` (`session.py:359`) | Counts the effective pool. |
| The papers table itself | `papers[]` from `distill` (`session.py:341-351`) | The excluded paper leaves the table and appears under "Left out". |

**Papers without a DOI.** They feed none of the first four rows above (the distiller and `concept_report` read DOI docs only), so excluding one changes only `n_docs` and the floor. They can still be excluded, because a sample full of no-DOI off-topic papers should be cleanable and refillable. The key for such a paper is its pool entry `id` (`provider|provider_id`, `recon/engine.py:116`), not a DOI.

## Refill: from papers already fetched, without a new search
**What the code allows today: nothing.** A probe asks every selected database for at most 25 records (`max_results=25` becomes the per-database cap, `scholar_search/engine.py:133-138`, `:161`), merges and removes duplicates (`:213-220`), and `probe_pool` then writes only the first 25 (`recon/engine.py:394`, `docs[:POOL_MAX]`). With four databases that is up to about 100 fetched records, deduplicated, of which at least 75 are discarded before anything is stored. Two consequences:
1. There is no stored reserve to draw a replacement from.
2. A refresh (`refresh: true`) does not help: it repeats the same request with the same cap and returns the same first 25 (a cached complete pool is returned without a request). The excluded papers would come back, and with exclusions applied the sample would only be smaller.

**What must change (✗ M10b, engine; the one change that makes refill possible).**
- `probe_pool` writes the deduplicated records beyond the first 25 into the pool file as `reserve` (in the order the sample was chosen), instead of discarding them. No extra request is made. The reserve is at most what the databases already returned; with the present cap that is about 75 records.
- `effective_pool(pool, exclusions)` = the first `POOL_MAX` (25) of `docs + reserve` that are not excluded. A paper that enters the sample this way is marked "Added to replace one you left out" on the Papers card (text badge), so a replacement is never silent.
- `pool_summary` gains `reserve_n` (records still in reserve) and `n_docs_raw`; `n_docs` becomes the effective count.
- A pool cached **before** this change has no `reserve`. For it there is no refill; the screen says "This pool was saved before replacements were kept. Search again to be able to replace papers." (a refresh stores a reserve; it is the same request, so it costs one search and the sample is the same 25).
- When the reserve runs out, the sample simply gets smaller; the floor recommendation below takes over.

**Dependency on M10a.** M10a changes how the 25 are chosen (each database's relevance order, spread across the selected databases) and adds database selection and order. This spec is written so that it holds either way: `reserve` is "the deduplicated records the probe fetched and did not put in the first 25, in the same order the sample uses", whatever that order is. If M10a does not land, the order is today's `stable_order` (`scholar_search/engine.py:217`). If M10a raises or splits the per-database fetch size, the reserve is simply larger. The exclusion keys (DOI or pool `id`) do not depend on M10a's choice of databases.

## Data out
- **Mark or put back:** `POST /api/v1/inception/drafts/{id}/recon/sample` ✗ M10b, body `{add?: [key], remove?: [key], base_updated_at?}` (`extra="forbid"`, as `BindBody`). `add` leaves papers out; `remove` puts them back. Both are idempotent: leaving out a paper already left out is not an error. Up to 25 keys per call. The answer is the new `sample` object (the screen then re-reads `distill`, `directions` and `concepts`; it never patches them locally, as `03` build note 4 says).
- **Where it is stored.** On the draft record, beside `grounding`, not inside `draft`: `record["sample_exclusions"] = {"items": [{"key", "doi", "title", "provider", "reason": "NOT_RELEVANT", "excluded_at", "pool_fingerprint"}]}` (✗ M10b, a new `DraftStore.set_sample_exclusions`, modelled on `bind_grounding` `drafts.py:95-110`: checks `base_updated_at` and `emitted`, writes `updated_at`). Reasons for this placement: the `Draft` model is `extra="forbid"` and a draft `PUT` replaces `draft` whole, so a field inside it would have to be carried by every screen's save (as `golden_seeds` is); like `grounding`, only the engine writes this and a later `PUT` keeps it. Every item records the fingerprint of the pool the researcher saw when marking it, so the genesis record can say what they saw.
- **Exclusions survive a new search** of the same draft: the key is the paper, not the pool. They are applied to any later pool of this draft that contains the paper (Open question 1 covers a changed question).
- **Limit:** 200 keys per draft (`SAMPLE_EXCLUSIONS_FULL`). A reserve and a pool total about 100 papers, so 200 allows several searches.
- **Binding.** A bound direction was computed on one effective pool. Marking or putting back a paper after the bind makes that direction out of date: `build_grounding` records `sample_exclusions_digest` (a hash of the sorted keys, computed by the engine) in `grounding`, and `check_binding` (`grounding.py:166`) adds a stale state `code: "SAMPLE_CHANGED"`, `reason: "the sample changed after this direction was chosen"`, beside `POOL_CHANGED` and `SCOPE_CHANGED` (✗ M10b). `STALE_BINDING` at preview and genesis already refuses on any stale state, so no new refusal is needed for it. The screen warns before the first mark after a bind (a dialog like the refresh warning in `02`: "Leaving this paper out will make your chosen direction out of date" with "Leave it out" and "Keep the paper", default focus "Keep the paper").
- **At genesis.** `recon_context()` (`grounding.py:252`) is what `genesis.py:336-341` writes verbatim to `audit/recon_context.json`. It gains (✗ M10b):
  ```
  "sample_exclusions": {
    "n": <int>, "reason": "NOT_RELEVANT", "decided_by": "researcher",
    "pool_n_docs_raw": <int>, "effective_n_docs": <int>, "refilled_from_reserve": <int>,
    "items": [{"key", "doi", "title", "provider", "excluded_at", "pool_fingerprint"}]
  }
  ```
  and `anchor_dois`, `anchored_terms`, `term_evidence`, `pool_sizes` are computed over the effective pool (today `pool_sizes` is `pool.n_docs` of the raw pool). The genesis journal event already carries a bounded copy of the context in its description (`genesis.py:343-359`); it gains `sample_exclusions_n`. I did not check whether `audit/recon_context.json` is covered by the Contract v1 baseline; `scripts/generate_contract_baseline.py --check` decides at build, and the milestone lane (STANDARD or RELEASE) follows from it.
- No other write. No journal event while the draft has no workspace.

| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `PAPER_UNKNOWN` (422; `details.keys`) | unclear | That paper is not in this sample | The sample may have changed since you opened the page. Nothing was changed. | Leaving a paper out | Reload the papers |

A second mark of the same paper is not an error. Leaving out the last paper is not refused (the sample is then empty and the empty-sample state applies).

## States
Roles: `include` for sufficient, `unclear` for below the floor or out of date, `blocked` for refusals, `exclude` (grey) for neutral facts. Status is always a text badge.

- **Default** (a sample, nothing left out): the Papers card is as in `02`; each row has a secondary button "Not relevant" (the DOI link and anchors column stay). Under the card title: "{n} papers · none left out". The directions count and all tiles are as before.
- **Papers left out** (`excluded` non-empty): under the table, a disclosure "Left out · {k}" (a plain card, solid `hairline` border). Each row: title (`dir="auto"`), year, provider, "Left out {date}", a DOI link and a button "Put back". The Papers card title reads "Papers · {n} · {k} left out". A paper that came in from the reserve carries the text badge "Added to replace a paper you left out". After each change the screen re-reads `sample`, `distill`, `directions` and `concepts`, and a polite status line says what moved: "Left out: {title}. Directions: {before} to {after}. Papers: {n}." The numbers come from the two reads, not from the browser's arithmetic.
- **Undo.** Pressing "Not relevant" shows a toast "Left out: {title}" with "Undo" for 8 seconds (`role="status"`, focus does not move; the toast does not time out while it has focus). Undo is `remove: [key]`; after the toast has gone "Put back" in the Left out list does the same. Both are single calls and always available until genesis; there is no limit on undos.
- **Marking a saved seed.** If the paper is in `draft.golden_seeds`, the first press opens a dialog: "This paper is one of the seeds discovery will look for. A paper you call not relevant is usually not a seed." with "Leave it out and remove it from the seeds" (does both: this call, then a draft `PUT` of `golden_seeds` without it, carrying every other field unchanged as `03` does) and "Keep it" (default focus). Nothing is removed unless the first button is pressed.
- **Marking after a bind.** The warning of "Binding" above. After confirmation the mark is saved and the stale note of `02`/`03` appears: "The direction you chose was based on an earlier sample." with "Choose a direction".
- **Below the floor (recommendation).** The two recommendations are checks of `02-checks`' catalogue, shown as rows of the checks panel on Explore and nowhere else: `EXPLORE_POOL_THIN` (`reason: BELOW_FLOOR`) and `EXPLORE_SAMPLE_MOSTLY_LEFT_OUT` (`reason: MOSTLY_OFF_TOPIC`); `sample.recommend` is the data both read. After exclusions and refill, if `sample.recommend.search` is true the panel shows the row in the `unclear` role above the tiles: "Only {n} of the papers are left. Searching again with a narrower or reworded question is likely to help more than keeping this sample." (`reason: BELOW_FLOOR`, `n_docs` under the floor `gates.pool.threshold`, 12, the same constant as the thin-pool tile) or "You have left out {k} of {total}. That many off-topic papers usually means the question, not the sample, needs work." (`reason: MOSTLY_OFF_TOPIC`, `marked_share` at or above 0.5 of the papers that were in the sample). One action, shown as a recommendation the researcher takes: "Change scope" (to `01`). A second, secondary link "Search again with semantic search" is offered only when the search was keyword. Nothing is run for the researcher. The card is advice: "See directions" stays enabled and the engine's refusal at bind (`POOL_TOO_SMALL`) is unchanged. The numbers 12 and 0.5 are engine constants (`SAMPLE_RECOMMEND_SEARCH_BELOW` = `POOL_THIN_FLOOR`, `SAMPLE_OFFTOPIC_SHARE` = 0.5, ✗ M10b); the browser compares nothing.
- **Empty.** (a) No usable pool: no control, as `02` says. (b) A pool exists but every paper is left out and the reserve is empty: the Papers card says "Every paper has been left out. Put some back, or search again." with "Put back all" (`remove` every key) and the recommendation card with `BELOW_FLOOR`. Directions, tiles and the seeds card follow the engine's empty answers (`directions: []`, thin), not an error.
- **Loading** (skeleton, `aria-busy`): the buttons of the row being saved are disabled with `aria-busy`, the other rows stay usable but a second change queues behind the first (one request in flight per draft; every handler ignores an answer for a superseded request, as `03` build note 2). The table keeps its previous rows until the reads finish, so rows do not jump.
- **Error** (typed refusal, `StateCard`; texts above and in `08`): `PAPER_UNKNOWN`, `SAMPLE_EXCLUSIONS_FULL`, `DRAFT_CONFLICT` (page read-only, reload offered), `DRAFT_EMITTED` (link to Done), `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`. After any refusal the table and the Left out list stay as they were; the row that failed shows its error beside its button and is not moved.
- **Partial.** A partial pool (a database failed, `pool.status` `partial`): works as before; the banner of `02` stays. The reserve of a partial pool is whatever the answering databases returned.
- **Offline** (engine unreachable): the loaded sample stays with "As of {time}"; "Not relevant", "Put back" and "Undo" are disabled with "needs the engine on this computer". Nothing is queued: a change that is not saved is not shown as saved.
- **Stale / protocol changed.** A draft has no protocol; the nearest cases: (a) *the question, years or languages changed after the pool* (`scope_changed`): the note of `02` applies; exclusions are kept and shown in Left out. (b) *A new search ran*: papers left out earlier that the new pool does not contain are listed in Left out with "Not in the current pool" (`in_pool: false`); they cost nothing and can be put back or ignored; the count "{k} left out" counts only those in the pool, and the list says how many are not. (c) *The pool was saved before replacements were kept*: the sentence under Refill. (d) *Bound direction out of date*: `binding.stale` with `code: SAMPLE_CHANGED`: "The direction you chose was based on an earlier sample." and "Choose a direction" (`03`).

## Desktop layout

## Keyboard
Focus order inside the Papers card: the sort buttons, then per row the DOI link then "Not relevant", then "Left out" disclosure, then each "Put back". After "Not relevant" focus moves to the next row's "Not relevant" button (or to the table caption when it was the last row), never to the page top; the toast is announced politely and Undo is reachable with `Tab` from where focus is and with `Escape` closing the toast without undoing. After "Put back" focus stays on the Left out disclosure button. In the two dialogs focus enters on the safe button ("Keep the paper" / "Keep it"), `Escape` equals it and focus returns to the row button that opened it. No single-key shortcuts.

## Accessibility
- Every row button names its paper through `mark.label` ("Not relevant: {title}"), so a screen reader does not hear "Not relevant" thirty times with nothing to tell them apart. The result of a change is a polite live region (`sample.moved`); the toast is `role="status"`; refusals `role="alert"`; the two dialogs are `role="alertdialog"` with the body as the description.
- "Left out" is a disclosure (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`). A left-out row is plain text plus the word "Left out {date}", never struck through alone or greyed alone (state is a word). The recommendation card is a labelled region in the `unclear` role with its words; no colour alone.
- Contrast pairs from tokens (`ink` on `surface`, `unclear` on `unclear-soft` only as the role's full colour text on a badge, `brand` on `ground`); a `-soft` tint never carries ink text.
- RTL (ar): the layout mirrors; the "Not relevant" button sits at the inline end of the row and the toast at the inline end of the viewport; Undo follows the message in reading order. DOIs, years, counts, fingerprints, keys and provider ids are `dir="ltr"` with Latin numerals; titles are `dir="auto"` (they may be in any language); the Left out date is formatted by the locale with Latin digits. Directions labels and terms are `dir="auto"`.
- Targets at least 44 px. No gesture-only action.
