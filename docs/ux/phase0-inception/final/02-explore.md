**Purpose.** Run the first search for the draft's question, see how healthy the resulting pool of up to 25 papers is (size, topical spread, schools, topics, metrics, datasets, papers), and decide whether to go on to Direction, search again, or change the scope. The pool is evidence the later steps draw on. Nothing in it is a conclusion.

`concepts` is called once per load and once after each finished search, only when a usable pool exists (before that the engine answers `RECON_NO_POOL`). `distill` on load runs only when the session has a usable pool (`session.latest` with `n_docs` above 0 and at least one earlier probe with a pool). ## Data out
- Start the first search: `POST .../recon/probe` `{refresh: false, semantic: <control>}` ✓ M4c `recon.py:236`, answers `202 {job}`. The topic, years and languages are read from the saved draft by the engine (✓ M4d for languages); the body carries none. M10a adds two keys ✗ M10a (decided, item 1a). `databases`: ordered list of ids from `openalex|semanticscholar|crossref|arxiv`; non-empty, no duplicates; an unknown, empty or duplicated list is refused `422 PROVIDER_UNKNOWN`. `query`: string, stripped, non-empty, at most 2000 characters, else `422 BODY_INVALID`; `narrower` together with `query` is also `422 BODY_INVALID`. The screen sends `databases` always (the picker's checked rows in their order) and `query` always when the field holds text (so the pre-filled derived query is sent as shown); an empty field sends no `query` and the engine derives one. The screen trims and limits the field before sending so these refusals are not reachable from normal use; they are still mapped. Unknown keys stay refused (422). The topic is still never in the body. The recorded probe carries `query` and `providers`, and each leg `executed_query`.
- Refresh pool: the same route with `{refresh: true, semantic: <control>}` plus the same two keys. It replaces the session's latest pool. If the draft is bound (`grounding` present), the screen warns first (see States, Refresh after binding) because the binding becomes stale.
- "Search again" after a partial or failed search is the same call as Refresh pool (`refresh: true`), so a cached copy is never what answers.
- Nothing is saved to the draft. No journal event: a draft is not a workspace.

## States
Roles: `include` for sufficient or coherent, `unclear` for thin, fragmented, partial or out of date, `blocked` for a refused or failed search, `exclude` (grey) for "no topic data" and other neutral facts. Status is always a text badge.

- **Default** (a complete, sufficient pool): eyebrow "Step 2 of 6 · Explore · the first search"; h1 "What do the first papers say about your question?"; lede "Pool health first, in plain words with the raw value underneath. The schools, topics and papers below are what the later steps draw on."; the Pool card (title "Pool · {n} papers", the line "fetched {created_at} · {keyword|semantic} · {year_min} to {year_max|present} · {languages}", "from cache" when `served_from_cache`, the short fingerprint, the button "Refresh pool"); three tiles (size, spread, your own words); the **Databases asked** card (key lines, one row per leg, leg notes); the **Your concepts in this pool** card (one line per concept); the heuristic-reading card (schools bars, topics chips, metrics and datasets chips); the Papers card; the button row (Change scope, Save draft and exit, "See {n} directions" brand).
- **Empty** (no probe yet, `session` null): the Pool card is replaced by the first-search card: the question as saved (`draft.topic`, `dir="auto"`), "{from} to {to or present}" with the default-year caption when the from-year is empty, the Keyword/Semantic control, the "Query sent to the databases" field (pre-filled with `derived_query`, see above), the database picker (all four checked in the order of `databases_available`, the engine's default openalex, semanticscholar, crossref, arxiv; replaces the old sentence that named the four in fixed order), the sentence "This sends your question to the databases you ticked and keeps up to 25 papers." (`first.body`), and the brand button "Search the literature". Above the button the two key lines show (from `keys`), so a missing OpenAlex key is seen before the search: "Not set — set SCHOLAR_OPENALEX_KEY" with the sentence "OpenAlex is still asked without a key and may still answer; once it refuses, that database is marked failed and the pool is partial." The years and languages line shows the draft's values ("{from} to {to or present} · {languages}"). Not an error; no code. If `draft.topic` is empty, the button is disabled and the sentence "Write your question in step 1 first." links to 01 (the engine would answer `DRAFT_INCOMPLETE` with `missing: ["topic"]`).
- **Picker and query states (M10a).** *Default*: as above. *Query edited*: the field differs from `derived_query`; "Use the derived query" shows and restores it. *Query too long or blank after trimming*: the field is trimmed before sending; over 2000 characters the screen shows the inline `unclear` message "The query can have at most 2000 characters." (`query.tooLong`), disables the search buttons and sends nothing (the engine's `BODY_INVALID` is the backstop). *Refusals from the probe*: `PROVIDER_UNKNOWN` and `BODY_INVALID` (422) map to their cards (table below); nothing was started. *None selected* (the researcher unticks the last one; the box may be unticked so the message can show): inline `unclear` message "Pick at least one database." (`picker.none`, `role="alert"`, linked to the group by `aria-describedby`), "Search the literature" / "Search again" / "Refresh pool" disabled with that reason; no request is sent. *Zero results*: a search where no leg fetched anything ends as `POOL_EMPTY` (the engine's code, not guessed here); the per-database table shows "0 results" per leg with each leg's query, and the hint "Six words joined by AND can find nothing; shorten the query above." (`legs.shorten`) with focus on the query field via the action "Edit the query". *Loading*: the picker and the query field are disabled while a job runs (`aria-busy` card). *Offline*: disabled with the rest. *Protocol-changed*: not applicable (a draft has no protocol); a pool searched with a different query or databases than the picker now shows is not flagged as stale by the screen, the engine's fingerprint check decides binding (item 1a: a different choice is a new pool). The picker reflects the latest probe on reload.
- **Loading** (`aria-busy`): after the 202, the card "Searching" with skeleton lines and a live list, one line per database as `leg` events arrive ("OpenAlex · 25 fetched"), announced politely; copy "Results are kept only if the search succeeds."; no cancel. On load with `active_job` set, the screen goes straight to this state and attaches to its events. The stepper and "Change scope" stay usable. When `done` arrives, the screen reads the pool (distill and directions) and shows Default or Partial. If the SSE closes without a terminal event, the screen polls `GET .../jobs/{job_id}` every 2 s; a `RECON_JOB_NOT_FOUND` (the console restarted; jobs live in memory) means it reloads `GET .../recon` and shows whatever the session now holds.
- **Error** (typed refusal, `StateCard`; en texts below):
  - Before the job (HTTP): `MAILTO_NOT_SET`, `RECON_JOB_ACTIVE`, `PROVIDER_UNKNOWN` (422, M10a), `BODY_INVALID` (422, M10a), `DRAFT_INCOMPLETE` (topic), `DRAFT_NOT_FOUND`, `DRAFT_ID_INVALID`, `DRAFT_UNREADABLE`, `RECON_SESSION_INVALID`, guard codes, `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`.
  - In the job (`failed` event, `job.error.code`): `PROVIDERS_FAILED`, `POOL_EMPTY`, `RECON_FAILED`.
  - Reading the pool: `RECON_NO_POOL`, `RECON_SESSION_NOT_FOUND` (cannot occur if the screen follows the rule under Data in; shown as its card if it does).
  - A failed or empty search keeps the answers and shows the per-database table (see Refusals). It is never cached; the next search runs fresh.
- **Partial**: two kinds.
  - *A database failed* (`pool.status` `partial`, one or more `legs[].status` `failed`, `n_docs` above 0): the pool is usable and shown, and a banner in the `unclear` role says "Some databases did not answer" and lists each failed one by name with its `error` (mono, LTR) and its `error_code` under Details (`PROVIDER_REFUSED`, `PROVIDER_RATE_LIMITED`, others as the engine sends them), plus the leg's notes verbatim. And "This pool was not saved for reuse; searching again asks all databases afresh." The Pool card is badged "Partial pool". Button "Search again" (refresh). The pool can be used to go on; the Direction screen shows the same banner.
  - *Thin pool* (`gates.pool.sufficient` false): the "Enough papers" tile becomes the `unclear` tile "Only {n} papers came back" with "Directions from fewer than 12 papers are often fragments." and raw "{n} of 12 needed"; actions "Search again with semantic search" (probe refresh + semantic true) and "Change scope" (to widen the years). "See directions" stays; binding on 03 needs a confirmation.
  - *Topics missing* (`gates.purity.label` `indeterminate`, no OpenAlex topics): the spread tile reads "No topic data" in the `exclude` role with the caption "Topics come from OpenAlex records; this pool has none, so spread cannot be judged." The topics chips are omitted.
- **Offline**: the engine is local, so offline is "engine unreachable". The loaded pool stays on screen with "As of {time}"; Refresh, Search and See directions are disabled with "needs the engine on this computer". During a job a dropped stream shows "Lost contact with the engine. The search may still be running." and keeps polling; nothing is queued.
- **Protocol-changed** (a draft has no protocol; the nearest cases):
  - *Scope changed after the pool*: `scope_changed` is true. Note in the `unclear` role: "You changed your {parts} after this pool was searched. ## As built (M10a)
Two pre-fill rules for "Query sent to the databases", settled in the M10a review (the rest of the field is as specified above):
- **The latest topic probe, never a narrower one.** On load the field and the picker take the `query` and `providers` of the latest probe whose `purpose` is `topic`. A narrower probe's `query` is a direction label sent as written, not a query for the question, so it is never the pre-fill, even when it is the latest probe.
- **A changed question takes the new derived query.** When `GET .../recon` reports `scope_changes` containing `topic`, the latest topic probe's `query` belongs to the old question: the field is pre-filled from `derived_query` (the new question's keywords) instead, and "Refresh pool for the new scope" sends it. The picker still takes that probe's `providers`. The screen compares nothing itself; it reads the engine's `scope_changes`.

Two more, from the last M10a UI review:

**Purpose.** Tell the engine which papers of the first sample do not belong to the question, so the directions, counts, synonym suggestions and seeds are drawn from the papers that do, and see at once whether the sample is still enough to go on.

**Why.** In the real run of 2026-11-13 the 25-paper sample was dominated by off-topic papers and the three proposed directions were generic. The researcher could see the papers but had no way to say "this paper does not belong", so every number on the later screens kept counting it. Nothing on this screen says which papers are off topic; the researcher decides, and the engine only stops counting them.

**Changed from the canvas and from `02-explore`.**
- **A per-paper control, new.** `02` lists papers with a DOI link and an anchors count and nothing else. Each row gains a text button "Not relevant" and, once pressed, an undo (see States). The canvas has no such control.
- **The excluded paper is not deleted from the pool.** The pool file is content-addressed and shared: `probe_pool` caches it by query key (`recon/engine.py:356-357`), so two drafts with the same query read the same file, and `pool_fingerprint` (`session.py:86`) is what a bound direction is checked against (`grounding.check_binding`). Writing exclusions into the pool would change its fingerprint, leak one draft's judgement into another and make "undo" a rewrite of a cache file. Exclusions are stored with the draft instead (see Data out) and applied when the pool is read.
- **"Searching again" does not refill the sample.** Stated plainly in "Refill" below: today a refresh returns the same 25 papers.
- **The thin-pool tile keeps its floor.** `gates.pool` (`POOL_THIN_FLOOR` 12) is computed from what is left after exclusions; the recommendation is a row of the checks panel (`EXPLORE_POOL_THIN`, `02-checks`), not a second floor for the same tile.
- **Machine output stays a draft.** Directions, schools, topics and synonym suggestions keep the draft styling of `01`; only the researcher's exclusions are written as the researcher's choices (a plain card, solid `hairline` border, no `-soft` fill behind ink text).

### Refill: from papers already fetched, without a new search
**What the code allows today: nothing.** A probe asks every selected database for at most 25 records (`max_results=25` becomes the per-database cap), and `probe_pool` keeps the 25 that `relevance_sample` (`recon/engine.py:173`, M10a) picks by rank across the databases in the researcher's order, deduplicated (`docs[:POOL_MAX]`, `recon/engine.py:453`). With four databases that is up to about 100 fetched records, of which at least 75 are discarded before anything is stored. Two consequences:
1. There is no stored reserve to draw a replacement from.
2. A refresh (`refresh: true`) does not help: it repeats the same request with the same cap and returns the same first 25 (a cached complete pool is returned without a request). The excluded papers would come back, and with exclusions applied the sample would only be smaller.

**What must change (✗ M10b, engine; the one change that makes refill possible).**
- `probe_pool` writes the deduplicated records beyond the first 25 into the pool file as `reserve` (in the order the sample was chosen), instead of discarding them. No extra request is made. The reserve is at most what the databases already returned; with the present cap that is about 75 records.
- `effective_pool(pool, exclusions)` = the first `POOL_MAX` (25) of `docs + reserve` that are not excluded. A paper that enters the sample this way is marked "Added to replace one you left out" on the Papers card (text badge), so a replacement is never silent.
- `pool_summary` gains `reserve_n` (records still in reserve) and `n_docs_raw`; `n_docs` becomes the effective count.
- A pool cached **before** this change has no `reserve`. For it there is no refill; the screen says "This pool was saved before replacements were kept. Search again to be able to replace papers." (a refresh stores a reserve; it is the same request, so it costs one search and the sample is the same 25).
- When the reserve runs out, the sample simply gets smaller; the floor recommendation below takes over.

**Since M10a (merged, PR #233).** The 25 are chosen by each database's own relevance, spread across the selected databases in the researcher's order (`relevance_sample`). `reserve` is the rest of that same ranked, deduplicated list: the records the probe fetched and did not put in the first 25, in the order the sample would have taken them next. No extra request is made. The pool's `sample` marker (`POOL_SAMPLE`, `recon/engine.py:97`) becomes `relevance-2`, so a new search of the same question fetches again and stores a reserve; a pool already in a draft's session keeps working without refill and shows the sentence above. The exclusion keys (DOI or pool `id`) do not depend on the choice of databases.

### States
Roles: `include` for sufficient, `unclear` for below the floor or out of date, `blocked` for refusals, `exclude` (grey) for neutral facts. Status is always a text badge.

- **Default** (a sample, nothing left out): the Papers card is as in `02`; each row has a secondary button "Not relevant" (the DOI link and anchors column stay). Under the card title: "{n} papers · none left out". The directions count and all tiles are as before.

- **Offline** (engine unreachable): the loaded sample stays with "As of {time}"; "Not relevant", "Put back" and "Undo" are disabled with "needs the engine on this computer". Nothing is queued: a change that is not saved is not shown as saved.
