**Purpose.** Run the first search for the draft's question, see how healthy the resulting pool of up to 25 papers is (size, topical spread, schools, topics, metrics, datasets, papers), and decide whether to go on to Direction, search again, or change the scope. The pool is evidence the later steps draw on. Nothing in it is a conclusion.

**Changed from the canvas.** Every cut or change:
- **"What do the first 25 papers say about your question?"** The pool holds "up to 25" (`POOL_MAX`, `max_docs` in the pool summary); the h1 and tile say "{n} of up to {max}", from `pool.n_docs` and `pool.max_docs`.
- **Fetch line "semantic · 2020 to present · English".** Kept in full since M4d (✓ M4d). The pool summary has `query`, `year_min`, `year_max`, `languages`, `semantic` and `created_at`; the probe now sends the draft's `start_year` (default 2000), `end_year` and `languages`, and the cache key includes them. The line reads "{mode} · {years} · {languages}" from the pool, not from the draft: `year_max` null reads "to present". A draft with no from-year is searched from 2000, so a caption says so (`explore.defaultYear`). The old cut "Language is cut" is removed. No database filters by language in the probe, so each leg's own note says "language (…): no language filter is sent to <db>"; the screen shows those notes verbatim (see Databases asked).
- **Databases asked (new card, M4d; the canvas has only a fetch line).** One row per provider in `session.latest.legs[]`: the name, the status in words, "{fetched} of {reported_total}" papers, the `error_code` under Details, the leg's `notes[]` verbatim, and "at least {n}" when `cap_hit`. Above the rows, the two key lines exactly as the discovery Plan screen prints them (`docs/ux/phase1-discovery/final/01-plan.md`, `apps/research-ui/components/discovery-plan.tsx`): OpenAlex "Set" or "Not set — set SCHOLAR_OPENALEX_KEY"; Semantic Scholar "Set, not yet verified" or "Not set", from `keys.openalex_key_set` and `keys.s2_key_set` (✓ M4d). A failed leg never reads as "no papers": it reads "failed", with its code and note.
- **Per-concept anchoring (new, M4d; the canvas's "Occurs in 4 of 25" belongs to Criteria).** A card "Your concepts in this pool" from `GET .../recon/concepts` (✓ M4d): one line per concept, "{concept}: in {anchored_in} of {n_docs} titles or abstracts". When the draft has no concepts yet, the engine returns the scope step's `default_concepts` with `source: "suggested"`, and the card says "Suggested from your question, not yet in your draft". No synonym suggestions are shown here; they are on Criteria (05). A concept in 0 records reads "not in the pool" in the `unclear` role (text).
- **Search mode (semantic).** The probe body has `semantic`. Offered as a two-option control (Keyword, Semantic) in the first-search card, default Keyword, sent only with the probe. It is a probe parameter, not a draft field and not a protocol field (01 keeps the draft free of it).
- **"Papers to sample".** Fixed at 25 by the engine (`max_results=25`; no body field). Not offered.
- **"Open pool file".** The pool file is a path on the engine's disk with no route to read it. Cut. The pool's short fingerprint (`sha256:` plus 12 characters, mono) and `created_at` are shown instead, as identity of what is on screen.
- **Tile 3 "Low prompt echo" badge ("above 0.3 on a focused pool is fine").** The engine returns `qei` as a bare number (fraction of the top-10 pool terms that echo the query words). It returns no label and no threshold on this route; `gates.py` documents QEI as a dispersion index that inverts on well-scoped seeds. No badge: a neutral tile "Your own words in the pool's terms: {qei}" with the caption "Share of the ten most frequent pool terms that repeat words of your question. A high share means the pool mostly echoes you; it is not a pass or a fail." If `qei` is absent the tile is not drawn.
- **School score "0.77" and the "thin · 1" badge.** `schools[{label, n, anchor_dois}]` has a paper count and no score or thin flag. Bars show `n` against the largest school's `n`; the count reads "{n} papers". The note "Federated learning rests on 1 paper…" becomes a static caption shown when any school has `n` of 1: "A school with one paper cannot become a direction on its own (a direction needs at least two papers)." The rule is the engine's (`_grounded_directions_for_terms` needs two anchor DOIs); the browser decides nothing.
- **"Enough papers" / "Mixed subfields" labels** map from the engine's `gates.pool.label` (`sufficient|thin`) and `gates.purity.label` (`coherent|fragmented|indeterminate`). The raw values are printed beside them, as the canvas does: `n_docs` against `threshold` 12; `top3_share` against `threshold` 0.50.
- **"+ 6 more" topics, "+ 21 more" papers.** Kept as disclosures. The topic chips are the distilled `topics[{label, n, score, anchor_dois}]`; `score` is not shown.
- **Papers table.** `papers[{doi, title, year, provider, directions_anchored}]`, sorted by the engine (anchors descending, then title). The column "Anchors" reads "{n} directions", "1 direction" or "none". Sorting by year or title is a display sort on loaded rows. A paper with no DOI shows "no DOI" and no link.
- **"See 3 directions".** The count is not in the distill answer. The screen also calls `GET .../recon/directions` once the pool loads and uses `directions.length`: "See {n} directions", "See 1 direction", or, with none, "See directions" (03 explains the none case).
- **"Add custom lexicon".** Cut, and it stays cut. The MCP tool `recon_distill` takes `lexicon_json`; `POST .../recon/distill` takes no body and uses the default lexicon. Resolved 2026-11-11 (M4d): no custom lexicon in the browser.
- **"Cancel search".** There is no cancel route. Cut. Leaving the page does not stop the job; coming back re-attaches through `active_job`.
- **Phase ladder in the app bar and "Not production" badge.** The shell owns them. Cut, as in 01.
- **`POOL_STALE` "Scope changed".** Not an engine code, but since M4d the engine reports it: `GET .../recon` carries `scope_changed` (bool) and `scope_changes` (a subset of `["topic","years","languages"]`), comparing the saved draft with the latest pool's `query`, `year_min`, `year_max` and `languages` (✓ M4d). The screen shows the engine's flag and names the changed parts from `scope_changes`; it compares no values itself. `topic_changed` stays on the route for compatibility and is not read. Resolved 2026-11-11 (M4d), Q3.
- **Machine output as a draft.** The schools, topics, metrics and datasets are keyword heuristics over the pool (schools can false-positive, see `_schools` docstring). The results card carries the draft styling of 01 (dashed `ink-muted` border, label "Heuristic reading · not yet yours") and the line "Counted from titles and abstracts by keyword rules." The pool health tiles and the papers table are evidence and use the plain card.

`concepts` is called once per load and once after each finished search, only when a usable pool exists (before that the engine answers `RECON_NO_POOL`). `distill` on load runs only when the session has a usable pool (`session.latest` with `n_docs` above 0 and at least one earlier probe with a pool). With no session, `GET .../recon` answers `session: null` (200); `distill` and `directions` before a probe answer `RECON_SESSION_NOT_FOUND` (404) or `RECON_NO_POOL` (409), so the screen does not call them until a probe has succeeded. A session whose last probe was empty has `latest.n_docs` 0 and no usable pool unless an earlier probe left one.

## Data out
- Start the first search: `POST .../recon/probe` `{refresh: false, semantic: <control>}` ✓ M4c `recon.py:236`, answers `202 {job}`. The topic, years and languages are read from the saved draft by the engine (✓ M4d for languages); the body carries none.
- Refresh pool: the same route with `{refresh: true, semantic: <control>}`. It replaces the session's latest pool. If the draft is bound (`grounding` present), the screen warns first (see States, Refresh after binding) because the binding becomes stale.
- "Search again" after a partial or failed search is the same call as Refresh pool (`refresh: true`), so a cached copy is never what answers.
- Nothing is saved to the draft. No journal event: a draft is not a workspace.

## States
Roles: `include` for sufficient or coherent, `unclear` for thin, fragmented, partial or out of date, `blocked` for a refused or failed search, `exclude` (grey) for "no topic data" and other neutral facts. Status is always a text badge.

- **Default** (a complete, sufficient pool): eyebrow "Step 2 of 6 · Explore · the first search"; h1 "What do the first papers say about your question?"; lede "Pool health first, in plain words with the raw value underneath. The schools, topics and papers below are what the later steps draw on."; the Pool card (title "Pool · {n} papers", the line "fetched {created_at} · {keyword|semantic} · {year_min} to {year_max|present} · {languages}", "from cache" when `served_from_cache`, the short fingerprint, the button "Refresh pool"); three tiles (size, spread, your own words); the **Databases asked** card (key lines, one row per leg, leg notes); the **Your concepts in this pool** card (one line per concept); the heuristic-reading card (schools bars, topics chips, metrics and datasets chips); the Papers card; the button row (Change scope, Save draft and exit, "See {n} directions" brand).
- **Empty** (no probe yet, `session` null): the Pool card is replaced by the first-search card: the question as saved (`draft.topic`, `dir="auto"`), "{from} to {to or present}" with the default-year caption when the from-year is empty, the Keyword/Semantic control, the sentence "This sends your question to OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref and arXiv and keeps up to 25 papers." (names from `legs` once known; the sentence is static copy for the empty state), and the brand button "Search the literature". Above the button the two key lines show (from `keys`), so a missing OpenAlex key is seen before the search: "Not set — set SCHOLAR_OPENALEX_KEY" with the sentence "OpenAlex is still asked without a key and may still answer; once it refuses, that database is marked failed and the pool is partial." The years and languages line shows the draft's values ("{from} to {to or present} · {languages}"). Not an error; no code. If `draft.topic` is empty, the button is disabled and the sentence "Write your question in step 1 first." links to 01 (the engine would answer `DRAFT_INCOMPLETE` with `missing: ["topic"]`).
- **Loading** (`aria-busy`): after the 202, the card "Searching" with skeleton lines and a live list, one line per database as `leg` events arrive ("OpenAlex · 25 fetched"), announced politely; copy "Results are kept only if the search succeeds."; no cancel. On load with `active_job` set, the screen goes straight to this state and attaches to its events. The stepper and "Change scope" stay usable. When `done` arrives, the screen reads the pool (distill and directions) and shows Default or Partial. If the SSE closes without a terminal event, the screen polls `GET .../jobs/{job_id}` every 2 s; a `RECON_JOB_NOT_FOUND` (the console restarted; jobs live in memory) means it reloads `GET .../recon` and shows whatever the session now holds.
- **Error** (typed refusal, `StateCard`; en texts below):
  - Before the job (HTTP): `MAILTO_NOT_SET`, `RECON_JOB_ACTIVE`, `DRAFT_INCOMPLETE` (topic), `DRAFT_NOT_FOUND`, `DRAFT_ID_INVALID`, `DRAFT_UNREADABLE`, `RECON_SESSION_INVALID`, guard codes, `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`.
  - In the job (`failed` event, `job.error.code`): `PROVIDERS_FAILED`, `POOL_EMPTY`, `RECON_FAILED`.
  - Reading the pool: `RECON_NO_POOL`, `RECON_SESSION_NOT_FOUND` (cannot occur if the screen follows the rule under Data in; shown as its card if it does).
  - A failed or empty search keeps the answers and shows the per-database table (see Refusals). It is never cached; the next search runs fresh.
- **Partial**: two kinds.
  - *A database failed* (`pool.status` `partial`, one or more `legs[].status` `failed`, `n_docs` above 0): the pool is usable and shown, and a banner in the `unclear` role says "Some databases did not answer" and lists each failed one by name with its `error` (mono, LTR) and its `error_code` under Details (`PROVIDER_REFUSED`, `PROVIDER_RATE_LIMITED`, others as the engine sends them), plus the leg's notes verbatim. Two cases get their own line from the note and the key state, never from the screen guessing: OpenAlex `PROVIDER_REFUSED` reads "OpenAlex refused the request; set SCHOLAR_OPENALEX_KEY" and a rejected Semantic Scholar key (`PROVIDER_REFUSED`, note naming SCHOLAR_S2_KEY) reads "Semantic Scholar rejected the key; it was not retried without it". And "This pool was not saved for reuse; searching again asks all databases afresh." The Pool card is badged "Partial pool". Button "Search again" (refresh). The pool can be used to go on; the Direction screen shows the same banner.
  - *Thin pool* (`gates.pool.sufficient` false): the "Enough papers" tile becomes the `unclear` tile "Only {n} papers came back" with "Directions from fewer than 12 papers are often fragments." and raw "{n} of 12 needed"; actions "Search again with semantic search" (probe refresh + semantic true) and "Change scope" (to widen the years). "See directions" stays; binding on 03 needs a confirmation.
  - *Topics missing* (`gates.purity.label` `indeterminate`, no OpenAlex topics): the spread tile reads "No topic data" in the `exclude` role with the caption "Topics come from OpenAlex records; this pool has none, so spread cannot be judged." The topics chips are omitted.
- **Offline**: the engine is local, so offline is "engine unreachable". The loaded pool stays on screen with "As of {time}"; Refresh, Search and See directions are disabled with "needs the engine on this computer". During a job a dropped stream shows "Lost contact with the engine. The search may still be running." and keeps polling; nothing is queued.
- **Protocol-changed** (a draft has no protocol; the nearest cases):
  - *Scope changed after the pool*: `scope_changed` is true. Note in the `unclear` role: "You changed your {parts} after this pool was searched. Directions drawn from it may not match." where `{parts}` is built from `scope_changes` ("question", "years", "languages", joined in words), and the pool line (query, years, languages) stays visible above for comparison. Action "Refresh pool for the new scope". The screen compares nothing itself (Resolved 2026-11-11 (M4d), Q3).
  - *The direction was chosen on an older pool, or the scope changed*: `binding.stale` true (reason `SCOPE_CHANGED` for a scope change, otherwise the pool reason). Note: "The direction you chose was based on an earlier pool." with "Choose a direction" (to 03). See 03.

`PROVIDERS_FAILED` and `POOL_EMPTY` carry `details.legs`: show a table (Database, Result, Fetched, Reason) with the legs' `provider`, `status` (text: "answered", "failed"), `fetched`, `error` (mono, LTR, as the engine wrote it), the `error_code` under Details and the leg's `notes[]` verbatim. This table is the same component as the Databases asked card. Provider ids map to display names through `inception.explore.provider.<id>` (openalex, semanticscholar, crossref, arxiv) and fall back to the raw id. For `POOL_EMPTY`, `details.status` is `empty`.

## Accessibility
- Tiles are labelled regions; every badge is text, never colour alone (`unclear` and `include` differ by word). Raw values stay as text under the badge.
- Progress list is `role="status"` with `aria-live="polite"`; the card is `aria-busy` until the terminal event. Refusals `role="alert"`.
- The schools bars are decorative; the number beside each is the content (`aria-hidden` on the bar). The papers table has a caption, real headers, sort buttons with `aria-sort`. DOI links name the paper ("{title}, DOI {doi}") and warn about the new tab.
- The heuristic card is labelled by its badge. Contrast pairs from tokens (`ink` on `surface`, `unclear` on `unclear-soft`, `brand` on `ground` for the brand button).
- The Databases asked card is a labelled region; each leg row states its status in words (answered, failed) and the key badges are text. The concept lines are a list; "not in the pool" is text in the `unclear` role.
- RTL (ar): the layout mirrors; DOIs, fingerprints, provider error strings and codes, env var names (`SCHOLAR_OPENALEX_KEY`, `SCHOLAR_S2_KEY`), the language codes, years, counts and `qei` are `dir="ltr"` with Latin numerals; the question, titles and school/topic labels are `dir="auto"` (they may be in any language); bars grow from the inline start.
- Touch and pointer targets at least 44 px (small buttons keep a 44 px hit area).
