**Purpose.** Run the first search for the draft's question, see how healthy the resulting pool of up to 25 papers is (size, topical spread, schools, topics, metrics, datasets, papers), and decide whether to go on to Direction, search again, or change the scope. The pool is evidence the later steps draw on. Nothing in it is a conclusion.

**Changed from the canvas.** Every cut or change:
- **"What do the first 25 papers say about your question?"** The pool holds "up to 25" (`POOL_MAX`, `max_docs` in the pool summary); the h1 and tile say "{n} of up to {max}", from `pool.n_docs` and `pool.max_docs`.
- **Fetch line "semantic · 2020 to present · English".** The pool summary has `query`, `year_min`, `year_max`, `semantic` and `created_at`; it has no language (the probe does not read `draft.languages`). Language is cut. The years come from the pool, not from the draft: `year_max` null reads "to present". A draft with no from-year is searched from 2000 (`recon.py` `draft.start_year or 2000`), so a caption says so (`explore.defaultYear`).
- **Search mode (semantic).** The probe body has `semantic`. Offered as a two-option control (Keyword, Semantic) in the first-search card, default Keyword, sent only with the probe. It is a probe parameter, not a draft field and not a protocol field (01 keeps the draft free of it).
- **"Papers to sample".** Fixed at 25 by the engine (`max_results=25`; no body field). Not offered.
- **"Open pool file".** The pool file is a path on the engine's disk with no route to read it. Cut. The pool's short fingerprint (`sha256:` plus 12 characters, mono) and `created_at` are shown instead, as identity of what is on screen.
- **Tile 3 "Low prompt echo" badge ("above 0.3 on a focused pool is fine").** The engine returns `qei` as a bare number (fraction of the top-10 pool terms that echo the query words). It returns no label and no threshold on this route; `gates.py` documents QEI as a dispersion index that inverts on well-scoped seeds. No badge: a neutral tile "Your own words in the pool's terms: {qei}" with the caption "Share of the ten most frequent pool terms that repeat words of your question. A high share means the pool mostly echoes you; it is not a pass or a fail." If `qei` is absent the tile is not drawn.
- **School score "0.77" and the "thin · 1" badge.** `schools[{label, n, anchor_dois}]` has a paper count and no score or thin flag. Bars show `n` against the largest school's `n`; the count reads "{n} papers". The note "Federated learning rests on 1 paper…" becomes a static caption shown when any school has `n` of 1: "A school with one paper cannot become a direction on its own (a direction needs at least two papers)." The rule is the engine's (`_grounded_directions_for_terms` needs two anchor DOIs); the browser decides nothing.
- **"Enough papers" / "Mixed subfields" labels** map from the engine's `gates.pool.label` (`sufficient|thin`) and `gates.purity.label` (`coherent|fragmented|indeterminate`). The raw values are printed beside them, as the canvas does: `n_docs` against `threshold` 12; `top3_share` against `threshold` 0.50.
- **"+ 6 more" topics, "+ 21 more" papers.** Kept as disclosures. The topic chips are the distilled `topics[{label, n, score, anchor_dois}]`; `score` is not shown.
- **Papers table.** `papers[{doi, title, year, provider, directions_anchored}]`, sorted by the engine (anchors descending, then title). The column "Anchors" reads "{n} directions", "1 direction" or "none". Sorting by year or title is a display sort on loaded rows. A paper with no DOI shows "no DOI" and no link.
- **"See 3 directions".** The count is not in the distill answer. The screen also calls `GET .../recon/directions` once the pool loads and uses `directions.length`: "See {n} directions", "See 1 direction", or, with none, "See directions" (03 explains the none case).
- **"Add custom lexicon".** Cut. The MCP tool `recon_distill` takes `lexicon_json`; `POST .../recon/distill` takes no body and uses the default lexicon. Open question Q1.
- **"Cancel search".** There is no cancel route. Cut. Leaving the page does not stop the job; coming back re-attaches through `active_job`.
- **Phase ladder in the app bar and "Not production" badge.** The shell owns them. Cut, as in 01.
- **`POOL_STALE` "Scope changed".** Not an engine code. The screen compares the pool's own `query`, `year_min` and `year_max` with the saved draft and shows a plain note when they differ (see States, Protocol-changed). Open question Q3.
- **Machine output as a draft.** The schools, topics, metrics and datasets are keyword heuristics over the pool (schools can false-positive, see `_schools` docstring). The results card carries the draft styling of 01 (dashed `ink-muted` border, label "Heuristic reading · not yet yours") and the line "Counted from titles and abstracts by keyword rules." The pool health tiles and the papers table are evidence and use the plain card.

`distill` on load runs only when the session has a usable pool (`session.latest` with `n_docs` above 0 and at least one earlier probe with a pool). With no session, `GET .../recon` answers `session: null` (200); `distill` and `directions` before a probe answer `RECON_SESSION_NOT_FOUND` (404) or `RECON_NO_POOL` (409), so the screen does not call them until a probe has succeeded. A session whose last probe was empty has `latest.n_docs` 0 and no usable pool unless an earlier probe left one.

## Data out
- Start the first search: `POST .../recon/probe` `{refresh: false, semantic: <control>}` ✓ M4c `recon.py:224`, answers `202 {job}`. The topic and years are read from the saved draft by the engine; the body carries none.
- Refresh pool: the same route with `{refresh: true, semantic: <control>}`. It replaces the session's latest pool. If the draft is bound (`grounding` present), the screen warns first (see States, Refresh after binding) because the binding becomes stale.
- "Search again" after a partial or failed search is the same call as Refresh pool (`refresh: true`), so a cached copy is never what answers.
- Nothing is saved to the draft. No journal event: a draft is not a workspace.

## States
Roles: `include` for sufficient or coherent, `unclear` for thin, fragmented, partial or out of date, `blocked` for a refused or failed search, `exclude` (grey) for "no topic data" and other neutral facts. Status is always a text badge.

- **Default** (a complete, sufficient pool): eyebrow "Step 2 of 6 · Explore · the first search"; h1 "What do the first papers say about your question?"; lede "Pool health first, in plain words with the raw value underneath. The schools, topics and papers below are what the later steps draw on."; the Pool card (title "Pool · {n} papers", the line "fetched {created_at} · {keyword|semantic} · {year_min} to {year_max|present}", "from cache" when `served_from_cache`, the short fingerprint, the button "Refresh pool"); three tiles (size, spread, your own words); the heuristic-reading card (schools bars, topics chips, metrics and datasets chips); the Papers card; the button row (Change scope, Save draft and exit, "See {n} directions" brand).
- **Empty** (no probe yet, `session` null): the Pool card is replaced by the first-search card: the question as saved (`draft.topic`, `dir="auto"`), "{from} to {to or present}" with the default-year caption when the from-year is empty, the Keyword/Semantic control, the sentence "This sends your question to OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref and arXiv and keeps up to 25 papers." (names from `legs` once known; the sentence is static copy for the empty state), and the brand button "Search the literature". Not an error; no code. If `draft.topic` is empty, the button is disabled and the sentence "Write your question in step 1 first." links to 01 (the engine would answer `DRAFT_INCOMPLETE` with `missing: ["topic"]`).
- **Loading** (`aria-busy`): after the 202, the card "Searching" with skeleton lines and a live list, one line per database as `leg` events arrive ("OpenAlex · 25 fetched"), announced politely; copy "Results are kept only if the search succeeds."; no cancel. On load with `active_job` set, the screen goes straight to this state and attaches to its events. The stepper and "Change scope" stay usable. When `done` arrives, the screen reads the pool (distill and directions) and shows Default or Partial. If the SSE closes without a terminal event, the screen polls `GET .../jobs/{job_id}` every 2 s; a `RECON_JOB_NOT_FOUND` (the console restarted; jobs live in memory) means it reloads `GET .../recon` and shows whatever the session now holds.
- **Error** (typed refusal, `StateCard`; en texts below):
  - Before the job (HTTP): `MAILTO_NOT_SET`, `RECON_JOB_ACTIVE`, `DRAFT_INCOMPLETE` (topic), `DRAFT_NOT_FOUND`, `DRAFT_ID_INVALID`, `DRAFT_UNREADABLE`, `RECON_SESSION_INVALID`, guard codes, `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`.
  - In the job (`failed` event, `job.error.code`): `PROVIDERS_FAILED`, `POOL_EMPTY`, `RECON_FAILED`.
  - Reading the pool: `RECON_NO_POOL`, `RECON_SESSION_NOT_FOUND` (cannot occur if the screen follows the rule under Data in; shown as its card if it does).
  - A failed or empty search keeps the answers and shows the per-database table (see Refusals). It is never cached; the next search runs fresh.
- **Partial**: two kinds.
  - *A database failed* (`pool.status` `partial`, one or more `legs[].status` `failed`, `n_docs` above 0): the pool is usable and shown, and a banner in the `unclear` role says "Some databases did not answer" and lists each failed one by name with its `error` (mono, LTR), and "This pool was not saved for reuse; searching again asks all databases afresh." The Pool card is badged "Partial pool". Button "Search again" (refresh). The pool can be used to go on; the Direction screen shows the same banner.
  - *Thin pool* (`gates.pool.sufficient` false): the "Enough papers" tile becomes the `unclear` tile "Only {n} papers came back" with "Directions from fewer than 12 papers are often fragments." and raw "{n} of 12 needed"; actions "Search again with semantic search" (probe refresh + semantic true) and "Change scope" (to widen the years). "See directions" stays; binding on 03 needs a confirmation.
  - *Topics missing* (`gates.purity.label` `indeterminate`, no OpenAlex topics): the spread tile reads "No topic data" in the `exclude` role with the caption "Topics come from OpenAlex records; this pool has none, so spread cannot be judged." The topics chips are omitted.
- **Offline**: the engine is local, so offline is "engine unreachable". The loaded pool stays on screen with "As of {time}"; Refresh, Search and See directions are disabled with "needs the engine on this computer". During a job a dropped stream shows "Lost contact with the engine. The search may still be running." and keeps polling; nothing is queued.
- **Protocol-changed** (a draft has no protocol; the nearest cases):
  - *Scope changed after the pool*: the pool's `query`, `year_min` or `year_max` differs from the saved draft's topic or years. Note in the `unclear` role: "This pool was searched for “{query}”, {years}. Your draft now says “{topic}”, {years}. Directions drawn from it may not match." with "Refresh pool for the new scope". The comparison is of two strings and two numbers the engine returned; the screen draws no further conclusion (Q3).
  - *The direction was chosen on an older pool*: `binding.stale` true. Note: "The direction you chose was based on an earlier pool." with "Choose a direction" (to 03). See 03.

### Refusals (M4c codes this screen can hit)
`StateCard`: sentence, what it affects, one next step, code under Details. Roles are brand status roles. Codes in 08 (`DRAFT_*`, guard codes, `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`) keep their 08 copy. The implementer registers the codes below in `STATES` (`apps/research-ui/lib/screening-states.ts`); fr and ar are written there, not here.

| Code (HTTP) | Role | en title | en body | en affects | en action |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `MAILTO_NOT_SET` (409) | blocked | This console has no contact email for the literature databases | A search needs one, and no request was sent. There is no setting for it in the app yet: set SCHOLAR_MAILTO in the environment or the .env of the console and start the console again. | New searches (a pool already on screen still works) | Reload the page |
| `RECON_JOB_ACTIVE` (409) | unclear | A search for this draft is already running | Only one runs at a time. | Starting another search | Watch the running search |
| `POOL_EMPTY` (job) | blocked | The search found no papers | Nothing was cached. Widen the years or rephrase the question. The per-database table shows what each answered. | This search | Change scope |
| `PROVIDERS_FAILED` (job) | blocked | Every database failed to answer | There is no pool. Nothing was cached; try again later. The per-database table names each failure. | This search | Search again |
| `RECON_FAILED` (job) | blocked | The search stopped on an unexpected error | The engine reports: {message}. Nothing was cached. | This search | Search again |
| `RECON_NO_POOL` (409) | unclear | There is no pool yet | A search has to succeed first. | Reading the pool | Search the literature |
| `RECON_SESSION_NOT_FOUND` (404) | unclear | This draft has not searched yet | | Reading the pool | Search the literature |
| `RECON_SESSION_INVALID` (422) | blocked | That is not a search session id | The draft link may be cut short. | This page only | Go to your drafts |
| `RECON_JOB_NOT_FOUND` (404) | unclear | The search can no longer be followed | The console may have restarted. The pool, if one arrived, is below. | This page only | Reload the pool |

`PROVIDERS_FAILED` and `POOL_EMPTY` carry `details.legs`: show a table (Database, Result, Fetched, Reason) with the legs' `provider`, `status` (text: "answered", "failed"), `fetched`, `error` (mono, LTR, as the engine wrote it). Provider ids map to display names through `inception.explore.provider.<id>` (openalex, semanticscholar, crossref, arxiv) and fall back to the raw id. For `POOL_EMPTY`, `details.status` is `empty`.

## Accessibility
- Tiles are labelled regions; every badge is text, never colour alone (`unclear` and `include` differ by word). Raw values stay as text under the badge.
- Progress list is `role="status"` with `aria-live="polite"`; the card is `aria-busy` until the terminal event. Refusals `role="alert"`.
- The schools bars are decorative; the number beside each is the content (`aria-hidden` on the bar). The papers table has a caption, real headers, sort buttons with `aria-sort`. DOI links name the paper ("{title}, DOI {doi}") and warn about the new tab.
- The heuristic card is labelled by its badge. Contrast pairs from tokens (`ink` on `surface`, `unclear` on `unclear-soft`, `brand` on `ground` for the brand button).
- RTL (ar): the layout mirrors; DOIs, fingerprints, provider error strings, years, counts and `qei` are `dir="ltr"` with Latin numerals; the question, titles and school/topic labels are `dir="auto"` (they may be in any language); bars grow from the inline start.
- Touch and pointer targets at least 44 px (small buttons keep a 44 px hit area).

## Build notes for the UI milestone
1. **Fix the step-bar hydration mismatch first.** A live review of the merged M4b screens found a P2 React hydration mismatch on every inception screen: `inceptionHref` in `apps/research-ui/components/inception-frame.tsx` builds the step-bar links from `window.location.search` (`?draft=`, `?console=`), so server and client render different hrefs and the console logs "Hydration failed". The new screens must take the draft id from `useSearchParams()` (or route params), or render those links after mount, and should not ship before that frame fix.
2. **SSE through the proxy.** Done in M4c: `STREAM_PATHS` in `app/api/console/[...path]/route.ts` carries `.../recon/jobs/{job_id}/events`, so the stream gets the caller's abort signal, not the 120 s read timeout (`console-proxy.test.ts`). Keep the polling fallback for a dropped stream.
3. Jobs live in memory of the console process (`ReconJobs`). A restart loses them; the session files persist. Always rebuild the screen from `GET .../recon`, and use the job only for progress.
4. `distill` and `directions` each re-distill the latest pool; call them once per load, not per render.
5. The browser sends only `refresh`, `semantic` (and, on 03, `narrower`, `followups`, `direction`, `own`, `accept_thin_pool`, `base_updated_at`); unknown body keys are refused (`extra="forbid"` → 422).
6. Provider names, statuses and engine messages are shown as the engine wrote them where they are data (error strings); sentences are the screen's own i18n keys, never the engine's English `message`.
7. Register the refusal copy and two new StateCard actions: `explore-search` (re-run the probe) and `inception-step` is reused (to 01 or 03).
8. Copy `inception.common.stepper.comingWithRecon` can be deleted once 02 and 03 are linked; the tests that assert "steps 2 and 3 are text without links" in 01, 04, 05 and 06 must be updated in the same change.

## Open questions
Q1. **Custom lexicon.** The canvas has "Add custom lexicon". The MCP `recon_distill` takes `lexicon_json`; `POST .../recon/distill` takes no body. Is a custom lexicon wanted in the browser at all? This spec marks it out of scope for the first build (the schools, metrics and datasets use the default lexicon, whose schools "can false-positive"). If wanted, it needs a new route (✗, a new milestone).
Q2. **Contact email.** The console has no in-app setting for `SCHOLAR_MAILTO`; the screen states this plainly and the researcher sets it outside the app. Is that acceptable for the first UI build, or should a setting (a write to the console's `.env`, a Contract-adjacent decision) come first?
Q3. **Pool older than the question.** The engine has no `POOL_STALE`: it does not notice that the saved question or years differ from the pool's. This spec shows a note from comparing the pool's `query`/`year_min`/`year_max` with the saved draft. Is a client-side comparison of those engine-returned values acceptable under "the browser makes no domain decision", or should the engine add a `scope_changed` field to `GET .../recon`?
