**Purpose.** Choose the direction the review pursues from at most three that the first search supports, or write your own, or go on without one, and bind the choice to the draft together with the evidence it rests on (the pool, its fingerprint and date, and the anchor papers). Then pick the papers that discovery's Recall check will look for. Directions are listed with their evidence; the screen never recommends one.

**Changed from the canvas.** Every cut or change:
- **"Listed by anchor count".** The engine does not order by anchor count: `_grounded_directions_for_terms` sorts by phrase length (longer first), then frequency, then alphabetically, and keeps at most three after a diversity filter. The lede says "in the engine's order (longer phrases first, then how often they occur)", and states that the order is evidence, not a recommendation. The canvas's "Direction 1" numbering is kept only as a label of position.
- **Nothing is preselected.** The canvas checks Direction 1. A preselected card would read as a recommendation (and a machine proposal is a draft, not an answer, as in 04). All cards start unchecked; the brand button is disabled until the researcher chooses.
- **"thin" badge on the 2-anchor card.** No engine field says a direction is thin; two anchors is the engine's minimum for any direction. The badge "{n} anchor papers" is a neutral chip (`surface-raised`, hairline) for every card, not a status role. The pool-level thin warning (fewer than 12 papers) is kept.
- **"Term occurs in 4 of 25 titles or abstracts".** Kept: `observed_in` of `n_docs`. It is the INC-21 anchoring rule (`term_evidence`): a paper counts only when the direction's term occurs in its title or abstract as whole words; "hits returned" by the databases is a different number and is never shown as support. A caption under the list states the rule.
- **Engine `detail` line.** Each direction carries `detail` ("pool term observed in {n} papers"). It is shown as a caption on the card, translated by key. The card's anchor DOIs and `observed_in` both follow the occurrence rule, so they always agree.
- **"Search again, narrower · 1 left".** The engine's narrower search is a probe on one direction's label (`narrower: <label>`), so it acts on the selected card; the button is disabled until a card is chosen. `narrower_left` (1 at the start) comes from `GET .../recon/directions`. The canvas's `DELTA_EMPTY` card says "Your original three directions are still available". That is true only when the narrower search finds nothing (it fails `POOL_EMPTY` or `PROVIDERS_FAILED` and the earlier pool stays the latest). When it finds papers, the narrower pool becomes the session's latest pool and the directions are recomputed from it; the first pool is no longer the one shown. Going back to the first pool is a probe of the question with `refresh: false`, which the cache answers without a request if the first search was complete (see States, After a narrower search). The narrower budget is not restored.
- **Optional gap check, follow-up cards, "Rephrase as follow-up A": all cut (decision 2026-11-11, researcher answer to old Q2).** The engine's `delta` runs up to three follow-up searches and merges their papers into a new pool, so it changes the pool that the synonyms (Criteria, 05) and the seeds (this screen) are drawn from, makes a bound direction stale, and keeps its results only in memory. The M4d screens need that pool to stay stable, so the gap check is not shown. The route `POST .../recon/delta` stays for MCP clients; the browser never calls it. The canvas's `DELTA_EMPTY`, `DELTA_PARTIAL` cards, the marker strip, the band badges and the "Search sparsity alone never proves a gap" note go with it. Resolved 2026-11-11 (M4d), Q2.
- **"Write my own direction … ungrounded until a concept check finds it".** The engine decides at bind time: `own` is recorded `grounded: true` only if the phrase occurs in at least two records of the pool (`observed_in` ≥ 2), else `grounded: false` with the DOIs it did find. New in M4d: a read-only check before binding, `GET .../recon/evidence?term=<phrase>` → `{term, hits, observed_in, anchor_dois, n_docs}` (INC-21 whole-word occurrence in title or abstract), shown by a secondary button "Check my phrase". `hits` is not shown as support (the same rule as the databases' hits: only `observed_in` of `n_docs` counts); the check is advice, and the engine still decides at the bind. Resolved 2026-11-11 (M4d), Q4.
- **"Continue ungrounded" (States `NO_ANCHORED_DIRECTION`).** Now a recorded choice, not a silent skip: the link "Continue without a direction" sends `POST .../recon/bind {"skip": true, base_updated_at}`, which writes `grounding {choice: "none", grounded: false, …}`; Review (06) and `intent.json` say the protocol was not grounded. The link binds the skip, then goes to Shape (04). Resolved 2026-11-11 (M4d), Q1. Its badge is the text "Not grounded" in the `unclear` role, never "ungrounded direction" (no direction exists).
- **Canvas `NO_ANCHORED_DIRECTION` and `POOL_THIN`** are not engine codes. They are screen states with no code (an empty `directions` list; `gates.pool.sufficient` false). The only codes shown are the typed refusals below.
- **Papers to check discovery against (new, M4d).** Not on the canvas. A card after the bind or the skip lists the pool papers that anchor a direction or a concept (`GET .../recon/seeds`), unticked, and saves the ticks as the draft's `golden_seeds`. See States and Data out.
- **Phase ladder, "Not production" badge, "Run from the terminal":** cut, as in 01.
- **Machine output as a draft.** The direction cards are the wizard's proposals: they use the draft styling of 01 (dashed `ink-muted` border, label "Proposed by the wizard · not yet yours"). The seed list is also a proposal and is styled the same until a paper is ticked. Once bound, the chosen direction is shown in a plain card as the researcher's choice, with its evidence.

## Entry / exit
- Step order: Scope 1, Explore 2, Direction 3, Shape 4, Criteria 5, Review 6.
- Entry: "See {n} directions" on 02; the stepper "3 Direction" (live); "Back to Direction" from 04.
- Exit: after a successful bind, "Continue to Shape" (brand) saves the seed ticks (if changed) and goes to 04. "Back to Explore" goes to 02. "Save draft and exit" goes to 08 (the bind has already saved the grounding; the seed ticks are saved first; with an unbound choice on screen it opens the leave dialog `inception.common.leave.*` modified to "Your choice is not in the draft yet. Choose it first, or leave without it."). "Continue without a direction" binds the skip and goes to 04 in the same action; if the bind is refused the page stays and shows the card.
- Gate: the binding button "Pursue {direction}" is enabled when exactly one card is chosen (or an own direction is written and non-empty) and no bind or search is in flight, and, for a thin pool (`gates.pool.sufficient` false), the box "I accept a pool this small" is ticked. The engine stays the authority (`NO_DIRECTION`, `DIRECTION_UNKNOWN`, `POOL_TOO_SMALL`). Nothing is written to a workspace. The only writes are the recon cache (narrower search), the draft record's `grounding` (bind or skip) and the draft's `golden_seeds` (seed ticks, saved before genesis, so they never change a protocol that screening is bound to).
- One brand button at a time: "Pursue {direction}" before a bind succeeds, "Continue to Shape" after.

## Data out
- **Bind**: `POST .../recon/bind` `{direction: <label>, accept_thin_pool: <bool>, base_updated_at: <draft updated_at>}`, or `{own: <text>, …}`, or `{skip: true, base_updated_at}`; exactly one of `direction`, `own` and `skip` (else `NO_DIRECTION`, 422). Writes `grounding` onto the draft record (beside `draft`, never inside it); a later `PUT` of the draft keeps it (the engine alone writes it). On 200 the screen stores the returned `updated_at` as `base_updated_at`. Binding again replaces the grounding. No journal event: a draft is not a workspace. Preview and genesis later read the grounding: `rq_evidence` and `concept_evidence` appear in the preview, and `audit/recon_context.json` is written at genesis (06 shows the direction choice, including "not grounded"; this spec only binds).
- **Narrower search**: `POST .../recon/probe` `{narrower: <label of the selected card>}`; the topic, years and languages come from the saved draft; the query sent is the label. One per draft (`NARROW_BUDGET` 1).
- **Own-phrase check**: `GET .../recon/evidence?term=<phrase>`; no write. Sent only on the button, never on each keystroke.
- **Seeds**: `PUT /api/v1/inception/drafts/{id}` ✓ M4a with `golden_seeds: [doi, …]` (max 25, nothing pre-ticked, DOIs only; every other field carried through as in 01), saved with "Continue to Shape" and "Save draft and exit", and by a secondary "Save seeds" button beside the list. At genesis the engine compiles them into `protocol.json` `search_strategy.golden_seeds`; the discovery Recall check pre-fills them (discovery `02-recall.md`).
- The "use this" rule: any button that copies a computed proposal into a field or state (here: choosing a card fills the "Pursue" label) is disabled while the request that computes the proposal is in flight, and ignores an answer that arrives for an older request (Shape inserted a stale sentence this way).

## States
Roles: `include` for a grounded choice, `unclear` for not grounded, thin, out of date, partial; `blocked` for refusals; `exclude` for neutral facts.

- **Default** (up to three directions, complete sufficient pool, nothing bound): eyebrow "Step 3 of 6 · Direction · pick one"; h1 "Which direction should this review pursue?"; lede "These directions come from phrases that occur in the titles or abstracts of at least two papers of your pool. They are listed in the engine's order, which is evidence, not a recommendation. You can also write your own."; a radiogroup of up to three cards (heading = the label, `observed_in` chip, "Term occurs in {observed_in} of {n_docs} titles or abstracts", `detail` caption, "Schools: …" or none, the first two DOIs as links and "Show all {n} DOIs"); the row (Search again narrower, Write my own direction); the pool line (fetched date, years and languages, "Partial pool" badge when applicable, "Back to Explore"); the link "Continue without a direction" with its note; the button row (Back to Explore, Save draft and exit, "Pursue {direction}" disabled until a card is chosen). The seeds card is not shown until a bind or a skip exists.
- **Empty**:
  - *No usable pool* (`session` null, or its probes all empty): the card "There is no pool to draw directions from" with "Go to Explore" (link). Not an error screen; no code is shown (`RECON_NO_POOL` or `RECON_SESSION_NOT_FOUND` would be the engine's words).
  - *No direction* (`directions` is `[]`): the card "No phrase occurs in two or more titles or abstracts of this pool. Nothing is invented as a direction." with "Change scope" (to 01), "Refresh pool with semantic search" (02's action; warns if bound), "Write my own direction", and "Continue without a direction" (binds the skip). Not a refusal; no code.
- **Loading** (`aria-busy`): skeleton cards while `GET .../recon` and `directions` load; the narrower search shows the progress card of 02 (live `leg` lines), with the cards inert and the buttons disabled. With `active_job` set on load, it re-attaches. The "Check my phrase" button shows "Checking" and ignores an answer for an older phrase.
- **Error** (typed refusal, `StateCard`; texts in 02 and 08 plus the table below): before a job `MAILTO_NOT_SET`, `RECON_JOB_ACTIVE`, `NARROW_BUDGET_SPENT`, `DIRECTION_UNKNOWN`, `RECON_NO_POOL`, `DRAFT_*`, guard codes, `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`; in a job `POOL_EMPTY`, `PROVIDERS_FAILED`, `RECON_FAILED`; on bind or skip `POOL_TOO_SMALL`, `NO_DIRECTION`, `DIRECTION_UNKNOWN`, `DRAFT_CONFLICT`, `DRAFT_EMITTED`, `RECON_NO_POOL`; on saving seeds `DRAFT_INVALID` (more than 25; `details.errors` under Details), `DRAFT_CONFLICT`, `DRAFT_EMITTED`. After any refusal the cards, the choice and the ticks stay.
- **Partial**:
  - *Pool partial* (`pool.status` `partial`): the banner of 02 naming the failed database, its `error_code` and its error; directions can still be chosen.
  - *Thin pool* (`gates.pool.sufficient` false): note in the `unclear` role "Only {n} papers came back. Directions from fewer than {threshold} papers are often fragments." with "Back to Explore", and the box "I accept a pool this small" next to the button; the brand button needs it. A `POOL_TOO_SMALL` on bind (the pool changed meanwhile) shows its card and the same box. The skip needs no box (no direction is claimed).
  - *Fewer than three directions*: the cards that exist; no placeholder cards.
  - *No fallback direction*: the CLI wizard can still build one "pool vocabulary" direction from several joined terms when no phrase occurs in two papers. Over HTTP a direction is offered only when its concept occurs in at least two titles or abstracts (`directions_for`, M4c), so that case reaches this screen as *No direction* (an empty list).
  - *Own phrase checked, under two papers*: the line under the field reads (`unclear`) "This phrase occurs in {observed_in} of {n_docs} titles or abstracts. A grounded direction needs two. You can still pursue it; it will be recorded as not grounded." with the DOIs found. At two or more, (`include`) "This phrase occurs in {observed_in} of {n_docs} titles or abstracts. It will be recorded as grounded." The words are the screen's; the numbers are the engine's.
  - *Own direction not grounded after the bind*: the result card is in the `unclear` role: "Your direction is recorded as not grounded: it occurs in {observed_in} of {n_docs} titles or abstracts, and a direction needs two." The researcher may continue or choose differently.
  - *Seeds*: none of the candidates is anchoring (`papers` is `[]`): the seeds card says "No paper of this pool with a DOI anchors a direction or a concept. You can add seeds by hand on the discovery Recall check." Not an error. Saved seeds absent from the current list are kept ticked as rows with the DOI only and the caption "Saved earlier; not in this pool"; the researcher can untick them.
- **Bound** (grounding present with a direction, `binding.stale` false): the chosen direction shows as a plain card "Your direction: {label}" with the grounded or not grounded text badge, `observed_in`, the anchor DOIs, the pool date and fingerprint, "Bound {bound_at}"; the radiogroup shows it chosen; the brand button is "Continue to Shape"; "Choose a different direction" re-enables the cards (a new bind replaces the old). Below it, the **seeds card** "Papers to check discovery against" (see Desktop layout).
- **Bound without a direction** (`grounding.choice` `"none"`): a plain card in the `unclear` role "No direction chosen · Not grounded" with "You went on without a direction. Review and the protocol will say so." and "Choose a direction" (re-enables the cards). The seeds card below, the brand button "Continue to Shape". The page goes straight to Shape after the skip action, so this state is seen on return.
- **Offline**: engine unreachable; the page keeps the last loaded directions with "As of {time}"; Pursue, Search again, Check my phrase, Save seeds, the skip link and Continue are disabled with "needs the engine on this computer". Nothing is queued.
- **Protocol-changed** (no protocol; the nearest cases):
  - *The binding is stale* (`binding.stale` true: a refresh or a narrower search ran after binding, the pool file changed or is gone, or the scope changed, reason `SCOPE_CHANGED`): note in the `unclear` role "The direction you chose was based on an earlier pool, or on a scope you have since changed." and, in Details, the engine's `reason` and the bound and current fingerprints (short). When `scope_changes` is present the note also names the parts ("question", "years", "languages"). Actions: choose again from the current directions (the cards are live; the old choice is shown as "chosen on the earlier pool"). Until it is re-bound, preview and genesis answer `STALE_BINDING` (06 and 04/05 show the shared `STALE_BINDING` card with the action "Choose the direction again", a link here). Seed ticks already saved stay saved.
  - *Draft changed in another tab*: bind answers `DRAFT_CONFLICT` (shared handling in 01).
  - *Before a search that replaces the pool*, when the draft is bound: a warning dialog (below) on "Search again, narrower", and on 02's "Refresh pool".
- **After a narrower search**: the cards are recomputed from the narrower pool and a line reads "These directions come from the narrower search on “{label}”." plus the action "Go back to the first search" (probe the question, `refresh:false`; it takes the cached first pool back as the latest without a request, only if that pool was complete; if it was partial the search runs fresh and the line says so). The seed candidates are re-read from the new pool. When the narrower search found nothing (`POOL_EMPTY` / `PROVIDERS_FAILED`), the original cards stay, and the narrower budget is shown as spent (`narrower_left` 0).

### Warning before a search that replaces the pool (bound draft)
Shown when `grounding` exists and the action will make a new pool the latest. Title "Searching again will make your chosen direction out of date"; body "You chose “{direction}” on the pool from {date}. This search replaces that pool. Your question and research questions stay, but you will choose the direction again before Review." Buttons: "Search again" and "Keep the current pool" (default focus). Nothing is sent until the first is pressed. When the draft is not bound, no dialog.

## Build notes for the UI milestone
1. **Fix the step-bar hydration mismatch first.** A live review of the merged M4b screens found a P2 React hydration mismatch on every inception screen: `inceptionHref` in `apps/research-ui/components/inception-frame.tsx` builds the step-bar links from `window.location.search` (`?draft=`, `?console=`), so server and client render different hrefs and the console logs "Hydration failed" on each load. The new screens must take the draft id from `useSearchParams()` (or route params), or render those links after mount, and should not ship before that frame fix.
2. **Computed proposals and in-flight requests.** Any "use this" button that copies a computed proposal must be disabled while its request is in flight (Shape inserted a stale RQ sentence this way). On this screen: Pursue and the card radios while a bind, a skip or a narrower search is running; every handler drops answers for a superseded request by request order (the own-phrase check too).
3. Send `base_updated_at` from the last draft read or bind answer. Store the `updated_at` the bind returns and the one a seeds save returns; a Scope or Shape save in another tab makes the next bind or save `DRAFT_CONFLICT`.
4. After any search that changes the latest pool, re-read `GET .../recon`, `.../directions` and `.../seeds`; never patch the cards locally.
5. SSE through the proxy: see 02 build note 2 (the recon events path is in `STREAM_PATHS`; keep polling as the fallback).
6. Sort order and counts are the engine's; do not re-sort the directions or the seeds, or compute anchor counts in the browser.
7. 04, 05 and 06 must render `STALE_BINDING` from `preview` and `genesis`, and 06 shows the direction choice including "not grounded" and the recall seeds kept (06); that is outside this screen and is listed so it is not missed.
8. Editing the question, years or languages in 01 after binding is reported by the engine (`scope_changed`, `binding.stale` reason `SCOPE_CHANGED`); the screen shows it and decides nothing. A skip binding is shown as the engine reports it.
9. The evidence answer's `hits` is carried in the response but not drawn; only `observed_in` and `n_docs` are support.
10. The gap check (`delta`) is not built in the browser; do not add a control for it.
