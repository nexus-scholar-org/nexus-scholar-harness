**Purpose.** Choose the direction the review pursues from at most three that the first search supports, or write your own, and bind the choice to the draft together with the evidence it rests on (the pool, its fingerprint and date, and the anchor papers). Directions are listed with their evidence; the screen never recommends one.

**Changed from the canvas.** Every cut or change:
- **"Listed by anchor count".** The engine does not order by anchor count: `_grounded_directions_for_terms` sorts by phrase length (longer first), then frequency, then alphabetically, and keeps at most three after a diversity filter. The lede says "in the engine's order (longer phrases first, then how often they occur)", and states that the order is evidence, not a recommendation. The canvas's "Direction 1" numbering is kept only as a label of position.
- **Nothing is preselected.** The canvas checks Direction 1. A preselected card would read as a recommendation (and a machine proposal is a draft, not an answer, as in 04). All cards start unchecked; the brand button is disabled until the researcher chooses.
- **"thin" badge on the 2-anchor card.** No engine field says a direction is thin; two anchors is the engine's minimum for any direction. The badge "{n} anchor papers" is a neutral chip (`surface-raised`, hairline) for every card, not a status role. The pool-level thin warning (fewer than 12 papers) is kept.
- **"Term occurs in 4 of 25 titles or abstracts".** Kept: `observed_in` of `n_docs`. It is the INC-21 anchoring rule (`term_evidence`): a paper counts only when the direction's term occurs in its title or abstract as whole words; "hits returned" by the databases is a different number and is never shown as support. A caption under the list states the rule.
- **Engine `detail` line.** Each direction carries `detail` ("pool term observed in {n} papers"). It is shown as a caption on the card, translated by key. The card's anchor DOIs and `observed_in` both follow the occurrence rule, so they always agree.
- **"Search again, narrower · 1 left".** The engine's narrower search is a probe on one direction's label (`narrower: <label>`), so it acts on the selected card; the button is disabled until a card is chosen. `narrower_left` (1 at the start) comes from `GET .../recon/directions`. The canvas's `DELTA_EMPTY` card says "Your original three directions are still available". That is true only when the narrower search finds nothing (it fails `POOL_EMPTY` or `PROVIDERS_FAILED` and the earlier pool stays the latest). When it finds papers, the narrower pool becomes the session's latest pool and the directions are recomputed from it; the first pool is no longer the one shown. Going back to the first pool is a probe of the question with `refresh: false`, which the cache answers without a request if the first search was complete (see States, After a narrower search). The narrower budget is not restored.
- **Optional gap check.** The canvas presents it as read-only advice with "Follow-ups used: 3 of 3 · served from cache after the first run". The engine's `delta` is not read-only: it runs up to three follow-up searches and **merges their papers into a new pool** (`pool_size_before`/`pool_size_after`, a new fingerprint, appended to the session's pools). It becomes the latest pool, so the directions can change and a bound direction becomes stale. There is no "used" counter across calls (the cap of 3 is per call) and the follow-up results are held only in the job in memory, not in the session. The card says so before it runs, and the follow-ups are not recoverable after a reload (Q2).
- **Follow-up cards.** `followups[{term, reason, confidence, school_n, corpus_total, saturation_label}]`. The count is `corpus_total` (works in the literature for that phrase, `-1` when it could not be counted) with `saturation_label` `scant` (under 50), `sparse` (50 to 500), `dense` (over 500) or `unknown` (the bands are the engine's: `engine.py:194`). The canvas's marker strip with "A · 31" is kept as an ordered, not-to-scale strip. The canvas's synthesized verdicts under each card ("Genuinely thin. Could be a gap, or a phrasing nobody uses.") and the summary note ("The narrow combination looks thin, but the parent fields are active") are interpretations that the engine does not make, so they are static explanatory captions per band only (`gap.band.<label>`), and the summary note is cut except for its last sentence: "Search sparsity alone never proves a gap: read the anchor papers before you commit."
- **"Rephrase as follow-up A".** Would rewrite the question; there is no route and the question is a Scope field. Cut. The follow-up phrase is shown selectable text.
- **"Write my own direction … ungrounded until a concept check finds it".** The engine decides at bind time, not at a later check: `own` is recorded `grounded: true` only if the phrase occurs in at least two records of the pool (`observed_in` ≥ 2), else `grounded: false` with the DOIs it did find. The screen says that, and shows the result after binding (`detail`: "your direction; it occurs in {n} of {N} titles or abstracts"). There is no read-only check before binding (Q4 notes this).
- **"Continue ungrounded" (States `NO_ANCHORED_DIRECTION`).** There is no route to mark a draft ungrounded without writing a direction. Skipping the step leaves the draft with no `grounding` at all, as in M4b: nothing is labelled "ungrounded", and Review (06) does not mention a direction. Offered as a link "Continue without a direction" to 04, not as a status. Q1.
- **Canvas `NO_ANCHORED_DIRECTION`, `DELTA_*`, `POOL_THIN`** are not engine codes. They are screen states with no code (an empty `directions` list; a job result with zero or fewer than three `followups`; `gates.pool.sufficient` false). The only codes shown are the typed refusals below.
- **Phase ladder, "Not production" badge, "Run from the terminal":** cut, as in 01.
- **Machine output as a draft.** The direction cards and the gap-check follow-ups are the wizard's proposals: they use the draft styling of 01 (dashed `ink-muted` border, label "Proposed by the wizard · not yet yours"). Once bound, the chosen direction is shown in a plain card as the researcher's choice, with its evidence.

## Entry / exit
- Entry: "See {n} directions" on 02; the stepper "3 Direction"; "Back to Direction" from 04.
- Exit: after a successful bind, "Continue to Shape" (brand) goes to 04. "Back to Explore" goes to 02. "Save draft and exit" goes to 08 (the bind has already saved the grounding; the button only leaves; with an unbound choice on screen it opens the leave dialog `inception.common.leave.*` modified to "Your choice is not in the draft yet. Choose it first, or leave without it."). "Continue without a direction" goes to 04 without binding (Q1).
- Gate: the binding button "Pursue {direction}" is enabled when exactly one card is chosen (or an own direction is written and non-empty) and no bind or search is in flight, and, for a thin pool (`gates.pool.sufficient` false), the box "I accept a pool this small" is ticked. The engine stays the authority (`NO_DIRECTION`, `DIRECTION_UNKNOWN`, `POOL_TOO_SMALL`). Nothing is written to a workspace. The only writes are the recon cache (narrower search, gap check) and the draft record's `grounding` (bind).
- One brand button at a time: "Pursue {direction}" before a bind succeeds, "Continue to Shape" after.

## Data out
- **Bind**: `POST .../recon/bind` `{direction: <label>, accept_thin_pool: <bool>, base_updated_at: <draft updated_at>}` or `{own: <text>, …}`; exactly one of `direction` and `own` (else `NO_DIRECTION`, 422). Writes `grounding` onto the draft record (beside `draft`, never inside it); a later `PUT` of the draft keeps it (the engine alone writes it). On 200 the screen stores the returned `updated_at` as `base_updated_at`. Binding again replaces the grounding. No journal event: a draft is not a workspace. Preview and genesis later read the grounding: `rq_evidence` and `concept_evidence` appear in the preview, and `audit/recon_context.json` is written at genesis (06 shows them; this spec only binds).
- **Narrower search**: `POST .../recon/probe` `{narrower: <label of the selected card>}`; the topic and years come from the saved draft; the query sent is the label. One per draft (`NARROW_BUDGET` 1).
- **Gap check**: `POST .../recon/delta` `{followups: 3}` (0 to 3 allowed; the screen sends 3; 4 is a 422).
- The "use this" rule: any button that copies a computed proposal into a field or state (here: choosing a card fills the "Pursue" label; "Use this phrase as my own direction" from a follow-up) is disabled while the request that computes the proposal is in flight, and ignores an answer that arrives for an older request (Shape inserted a stale sentence this way).

## States
Roles: `include` for a grounded choice, `unclear` for ungrounded, thin, out of date, partial; `blocked` for refusals; `exclude` for neutral facts.

- **Default** (up to three directions, complete sufficient pool, nothing bound): eyebrow "Step 3 of 6 · Direction · pick one"; h1 "Which direction should this review pursue?"; lede "These directions come from phrases that occur in the titles or abstracts of at least two papers of your pool. They are listed in the engine's order, which is evidence, not a recommendation. You can also write your own."; a radiogroup of up to three cards (heading = the label, `observed_in` chip, "Term occurs in {observed_in} of {n_docs} titles or abstracts", `detail` caption, "Schools: …" or none, the first two DOIs as links and "Show all {n} DOIs"); the row (Search again narrower, Write my own direction); the pool line (fetched date, "Partial pool" badge when applicable, "Back to Explore"); the gap-check card; the button row (Back to Explore, Save draft and exit, "Pursue {direction}" disabled until a card is chosen).
- **Empty**:
  - *No usable pool* (`session` null, or its probes all empty): the card "There is no pool to draw directions from" with "Go to Explore" (link). Not an error screen; no code is shown (`RECON_NO_POOL` or `RECON_SESSION_NOT_FOUND` would be the engine's words).
  - *No direction* (`directions` is `[]`): the card "No phrase occurs in two or more titles or abstracts of this pool. Nothing is invented as a direction." with "Change scope" (to 01), "Refresh pool with semantic search" (02's action; warns if bound), "Write my own direction", and "Continue without a direction" (Q1). Not a refusal; no code.
- **Loading** (`aria-busy`): skeleton cards while `GET .../recon` and `directions` load; the narrower search and the gap check show the progress card of 02 (live `leg` lines for the narrower search; for the gap check, the statement "Checking up to three follow-up phrases" with a skeleton, since `delta` emits no `leg` events), with the cards inert and the buttons disabled. With `active_job` set on load, it re-attaches.
- **Error** (typed refusal, `StateCard`; texts in 02 and 08 plus the table below): before a job `MAILTO_NOT_SET`, `RECON_JOB_ACTIVE`, `NARROW_BUDGET_SPENT`, `DIRECTION_UNKNOWN`, `RECON_NO_POOL`, `DRAFT_*`, guard codes, `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`; in a job `POOL_EMPTY`, `PROVIDERS_FAILED`, `RECON_FAILED`; on bind `POOL_TOO_SMALL`, `NO_DIRECTION`, `DIRECTION_UNKNOWN`, `DRAFT_CONFLICT`, `DRAFT_EMITTED`, `RECON_NO_POOL`. After any refusal the cards and the choice stay.
- **Partial**:
  - *Pool partial* (`pool.status` `partial`): the banner of 02 naming the failed database and its error; directions can still be chosen.
  - *Thin pool* (`gates.pool.sufficient` false): note in the `unclear` role "Only {n} papers came back. Directions from fewer than {threshold} papers are often fragments." with "Back to Explore", and the box "I accept a pool this small" next to the button; the brand button needs it. A `POOL_TOO_SMALL` on bind (the pool changed meanwhile) shows its card and the same box.
  - *Fewer than three directions*: the cards that exist; no placeholder cards.
  - *No fallback direction*: the CLI wizard can still build one "pool vocabulary" direction from several joined terms when no phrase occurs in two papers. Over HTTP a direction is offered only when its concept occurs in at least two titles or abstracts (`directions_for` in `recon/session.py`, M4c; resolved Q5), so that case reaches this screen as *No direction* (an empty list).
  - *Own direction ungrounded*: after binding `own` with `grounded: false`, the result card is in the `unclear` role: "Your direction is recorded as ungrounded: it occurs in {observed_in} of {n_docs} titles or abstracts, and a direction needs two." The researcher may continue or choose differently.
  - *Gap check partial*: `followups` has fewer than three entries (the engine returns follow-ups only for thin schools): the card shows those and says "{n} follow-ups were run. Read them as incomplete, not as a gap." There is no per-follow-up failure state in the engine; a failed job is `RECON_FAILED`.
- **Bound** (grounding present, `binding.stale` false): the chosen direction shows as a plain card "Your direction: {label}" with the grounded or ungrounded text badge, `observed_in`, the anchor DOIs, the pool date and fingerprint, "Bound {bound_at}"; the radiogroup shows it chosen; the brand button is "Continue to Shape"; "Choose a different direction" re-enables the cards (a new bind replaces the old).
- **Offline**: engine unreachable; the page keeps the last loaded directions with "As of {time}"; Pursue, Search again, Gap check and Continue are disabled with "needs the engine on this computer". Nothing is queued.
- **Protocol-changed** (no protocol; the nearest cases):
  - *The binding is stale* (`binding.stale` true: a refresh, a narrower search or a gap check ran after binding, or the pool file changed or is gone): note in the `unclear` role "The direction you chose was based on an earlier pool." and, in Details, the engine's `reason` and the bound and current fingerprints (short). Actions: choose again from the current directions (the cards are live; the old choice is shown as "chosen on the earlier pool"). Until it is re-bound, preview and genesis answer `STALE_BINDING` (06 and 04/05 show the shared `STALE_BINDING` card with the action "Choose the direction again", a link here).
  - *Draft changed in another tab*: bind answers `DRAFT_CONFLICT` (shared handling in 01).
  - *Before any search that replaces the pool*, when the draft is bound: a warning dialog (below) on "Search again, narrower" and on the gap check, and on 02's "Refresh pool".
- **After a narrower search**: the cards are recomputed from the narrower pool and a line reads "These directions come from the narrower search on “{label}”." plus the action "Go back to the first search" (probe the question, `refresh:false`; it takes the cached first pool back as the latest without a request, only if that pool was complete; if it was partial the search runs fresh and the line says so). When the narrower search found nothing (`POOL_EMPTY` / `PROVIDERS_FAILED`), the original cards stay, and the narrower budget is shown as spent (`narrower_left` 0).

### Warning before a search that replaces the pool (bound draft)
Shown when `grounding` exists and the action will make a new pool the latest. Title "Searching again will make your chosen direction out of date"; body "You chose “{direction}” on the pool from {date}. This search replaces that pool. Your question and research questions stay, but you will choose the direction again before Review." Buttons: "Search again" (or "Run the gap check", which adds "It also adds papers to the pool." to the body) and "Keep the current pool" (default focus). Nothing is sent until the first is pressed. When the draft is not bound, no dialog.

### Refusals (M4c codes this screen can hit beyond 02's)
Same `StateCard` rules and roles as 02; registered in `STATES`.

## Keyboard
Focus order: skip link, shell, stepper, h1, the direction radiogroup (arrow keys move, `Space` chooses; inside a card the DOI links follow the radio in Tab order), "Show all DOIs" buttons, Search again narrower, Write my own direction (and its field), the thin-pool box when shown, Run the gap check, follow-up cards (text), Back to Explore, Save draft and exit, Pursue or Continue. Choosing a card moves focus nowhere. After a bind succeeds, focus moves to the result card heading and the brand button becomes "Continue to Shape". After a job starts, focus goes to the progress card; after it ends to the cards' heading (new cards) or to the refusal card. The warning dialog opens on "Keep the current pool"; `Escape` is the same; focus returns to the button that opened it. No single-key shortcuts.

## Accessibility
- The directions are a `role="radiogroup"` labelled "Directions"; each card a labelled radio whose description is its occurrence sentence and detail; DOI links inside a card are outside the radio's hit area for activation, so choosing a card never follows a link. - Count chips, the grounded and ungrounded words, the band words (scant, sparse, dense) and the stale and thin notes are text. The strip markers carry text labels ("A · 31"); the strip is `aria-hidden` and the follow-up cards are the content.
- The thin-pool box is a labelled checkbox; the disabled Pursue button has `aria-describedby` pointing at the reason ("Choose a direction", "Accept a small pool", "A search is running").
- Refusals `role="alert"`; progress `role="status"`; "Bound" result announced with `aria-live="polite"`. The warning dialog is `role="alertdialog"` with its body as the description.
- Contrast pairs from tokens. RTL (ar): the card grid mirrors; DOIs, fingerprints and counts are `dir="ltr"` with Latin numerals; direction labels, follow-up phrases and the own-direction field are `dir="auto"`.

## Open questions
Q1. **Going on without a direction.** The canvas offers "Continue ungrounded" and promises it is "labelled as such through Review and in `intent.json`". The engine has no route for that: only a bound `own` direction is recorded as ungrounded. Skipping leaves no grounding at all and Review says nothing. Do you want (a) the skip link as specified here, with that consequence stated on screen, (b) no skip (the researcher must bind a direction or an own direction), or (c) an engine change so a skip is recorded as an explicit ungrounded choice?
Q2. **The gap check changes the pool.** The engine merges the follow-up papers into a new latest pool, makes a bound direction stale, has no cross-call "used" counter and does not keep the follow-up results after a reload. This spec runs it as an explicit, warned action and says the results are not kept. Do you want it limited to once per draft (the screen could infer "already run" from a `delta` probe in the session), shown at all in the first build, or kept read-only by changing the engine so it does not merge?
Q3. **Question or years edited after binding.** Saving a new question in 01 leaves the grounding in place, and the engine reports `stale` only when the pool changes. Should a changed question or years mark the binding out of date (an engine rule), or is the researcher trusted to rebind? (Related to 02 Q3.)
Q4. **Checking your own direction before it is bound.** The occurrence count for "Write my own direction" is known only by binding (there is no read-only route), so the researcher sees "ungrounded" after the write, and can bind again to replace it. Is that acceptable, or should a read-only check route be added first?
Q5. *Resolved in M4c (engine).* The fallback "pool vocabulary" direction is no longer offered over HTTP: a direction needs its concept to occur in at least two titles or abstracts, and its anchors are those papers (`tests/recon/test_session.py::test_a_direction_is_offered_only_when_its_term_occurs_in_two_records`). The CLI wizard keeps it until prior finding 9 is fixed there.
