**Purpose.** After the protocol is frozen and before a corpus is accepted, change the search the same way it is developed at Inception (edit, count, compare, check the known papers), and record the change as an amendment with a reason and a clear list of what it makes obsolete.

**Changed from the canvas and from `01-plan`.** This spec amends 01; it does not rewrite it. Everything else on Plan (executed strings, database picker, limits, contact email, the estimate card, the next links) is as 01.
- **Plan gains one secondary action, "Change the search"**, which opens the workbench on the protocol's query. 01 said the Plan has no write. The one write is the amendment, behind an explicit confirmation; Plan still sends nothing to the databases until the researcher presses Count in the workbench (01's "No auto-count" rule holds).
- **01's link "Change the protocol through inception" stays** for everything that is not the search (criteria, title, matrix; 01 open question 2). Its help hint `plan.amend.help` ("A different query or database is a protocol amendment, recorded by inception.") is no longer true for the query: the new key `plan.search.help` replaces it for the query part (see Copy and Open question 7). The estimate's "narrow" recommendation (`estimate.rec.narrow.go`) points to the same place; whether it now points to "Change the search" is Open question 7.
- **One brand button per screen.** Plan has none (its exits are secondary). While the amendment confirmation is open, "Record this amendment" is the screen's one brand button (it is a write, the screen's gate); closed, Plan has none again. Never two.
- **No frame, no blocks.** Plan is not part of direction A (the redesign covers Inception only). See "Where this follows or changes direction A".
- **Machine output is a draft.** Counts, strings, suggestions and reports are the engine's reading, in the dashed draft styling with source and date. The amendment is the researcher's act and says so ("recorded by you, with this reason").
- **After a corpus is accepted the workbench is read-only** (see States).
- **Phone.** As 01: desktop only.

## Where this follows or changes direction A
The Inception redesign (direction A, "the protocol sheet", D1 to D15, decided 2026-11-20) covers Inception. **Plan is not part of A**: it keeps its own page, its step bar and the next-step bar. So:
- **Followed (the workbench inside it):** the concepts block is one block, open or written (D13); the evidence column holds counts, known papers and comparison sets (D10 budget measured on that region; at most 6 hint buttons); every suggestion has one look (D5: "Suggested by {source} · not yet yours", Apply and Reject per term, no Apply all) with sources "a known paper" and "the {database} comparison" (there is no first-search pool on Plan); keys only while a list row has focus, Ctrl+Enter the primary action (D9); synonyms as wrapping chips.
- **Not changed:** D2 (no next-step bar on `/inception`) does not apply here: Plan keeps the next-step bar. D7: saving the draft is an Inception matter; here the one write is the amendment. D11 (Explore's fold) is not touched. M11a does not build A's frame (M10f-1).
- **Changed on Plan (not an A matter):** the decision bar of the workbench has one primary action at a time (Count, then Record this amendment), and the brand button rule is stated above.

## Entry / exit
- Entry: the secondary button "Change the search" in the header of the "Executed string per database" card; a link "Change the search" from the Hand-off's `HANDOFF_SEARCH_CHANGED` row and from the Recall page's seed-missed row (`RECALL_SEED_MISSED` points to "amend the query"; today it goes to Inception; the amended target is Open question 7).
- Exit: "Close" returns to Plan as 01 draws it. "Continue to Recall" and "Continue to Harvest with this choice" are unchanged; a recorded amendment ends with Plan re-read (new fingerprint, new strings). - Gate: Record this amendment is disabled until the version differs from the protocol's, the reason has one sentence, and the confirmation was read (the list is on screen, not behind a click). Counting is never a condition. No check is a blocker.

## States
Row states, the counts table, the seed check and the comparison set have the exact words of `10-query-workbench` "States" (counting, counted, cached, upper bound, previous version, quota used up, budget spent, busy, refused, unavailable, timeout, key missing, not countable, not asked, Crossref not comparable and "Count anyway" with "any of the words, not your query", seeds too easy and its per-concept and too-few variants, no seeds, comparison staged, measured, refused). This section states what is particular to Plan.

- **Default**: Plan as 01. The header of the "Executed string per database" card gains the secondary button "Change the search". Closed, the workbench is not drawn. Opened, it is a full-width region above the main and side columns (the executed strings and the Limits and Estimate cards stay below, unchanged), headed "Search" with "Search: main" (`workbench.queryKey`; the slot M3j will use for a selector). Two labelled groups: "In the protocol" (the fingerprint, the saved version's chips, read-only) and "This version" (the editable chips). The workbench opens on the protocol's query, counted nothing: "Nothing counted yet. Count asks each database how many records match; no records are fetched." Nothing is counted on open.
- **Comparison measure when a harvest exists.** With a run, the strongest measure is used: "Compared with {run}, {n} records, by DOI or normalised title." (`plan.search.cmp.harvest`), no request. For the run: "31 of 130 Scopus records are missing from what your query finds." The report still lists the missing titles, 20 per page, the concepts each lacks, and the terms (each "in 9 of 31 missing records") with Apply and Reject per term. Without a run: "No harvest yet, so this compares with OpenAlex (opt-in, cost shown) or with the words of your query." (`plan.search.cmp.noHarvest`) and the two other measures of 10-query-workbench. After acceptance: "Compared with the accepted corpus, {n} works, by DOI or normalised title." (`plan.search.cmp.corpus`).
- **Known papers on Plan.** The protocol's `golden_seeds`, read-only (seeds are not amended here; Open question 5 of 10-query-workbench applies to Inception only). Per database where one request answers it: OpenAlex found or not found; others "Not checked here. The recall check on Plan runs the full query." (the recall check on its own screen, 02-recall, is unchanged).
- **No first-search pool on Plan.** Per-term "in n of 25" and the first-search suggestions are not shown (a workspace has no live pool; Open question 8). Suggestions come only from known papers and comparison sets.
- **Empty**: (1) a protocol with no concept cannot occur after genesis (`CONCEPTS_NONE` blocks genesis); if the read returns none, "This protocol holds no concepts, so there is nothing to change here. Change the protocol through inception." (`plan.search.noConcepts`). (2) No staged export and no run: the comparison section reads "No export staged. Add one from Scopus, Web of Science or another database." (3) The consequences list with nothing to list: "Nothing else depends on the earlier query." (`plan.search.conseq.none`).
- **Loading** (skeleton, `aria-busy`): as 01 for the page; as 10-query-workbench for counting ("Counting…" per row; earlier numbers dimmed with "As of {time}"); while the consequences list loads: "Reading what this amendment changes…" (`plan.search.conseq.loading`), Record this amendment disabled.
- **Error** (typed refusal, `StateCard`; the words below are proposed, the codes exist unless marked):
  - `WORKBENCH_BUDGET_SPENT` (✗ part A): the row says "Today's counting budget for {database} is used ({n} requests). It renews at {time} UTC. The harvest's quota is kept for the harvest."
  - `WORKBENCH_VERSION_INVALID` (✗ part A): "This version cannot be searched: {error}." (cannot be provoked by the UI).
  - `DISCOVERY_JOB_ACTIVE` (409): on Count: "A search or a recall check is running on this computer, so counting waits. The databases' rate limits are its." (`workbench.err.jobActive`); on Record this amendment: "A {kind} is running in this workspace, so the search cannot be amended now. Wait for it to end or stop it." (`plan.search.err.jobActive`) with "Follow the running job" (the shared action). Nothing is written.
  - `PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT_REFUSED` with `details.reason` `outside_search`: "The engine only amends the search here, and this change reaches something else. Nothing was changed." (`plan.search.err.outside`); the engine's message under Details. Cannot be provoked by the UI (it sends concepts only); shown if seen.
  - `CORPUS_ALREADY_ACCEPTED` (409), on a stale tab: the read-only state below replaces the workbench; the shared card says "The corpus is frozen, so nothing is searched or imported any more. Screening decisions depend on it." with "Back to screening".
  - `MAILTO_NOT_SET`, `OPENALEX_KEY_NOT_SET`: the exact lines and "Check again", as 01 and the estimate; other databases can still be counted.
  - `PROTOCOL_MISSING`, `PROTOCOL_INVALID`: as 01 (whole-screen cards).
  - The reason missing or too long on Record: the field is `aria-invalid` with "Write one sentence." (`plan.search.reason.need`); the engine's refusal for an empty or over-long reason is named at build (Open question 4).
  - `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`: see Offline.
- **Partial**: some databases counted, some not (as 10-query-workbench); a recorded amendment whose new version was never counted is allowed and the journal says "not counted" (Open question 5). Some known papers not read; a comparison set with records without an abstract (counted apart).
- **Offline**: the engine is local; offline is unreachable. Chips edit in the tab; every engine action is disabled with "needs the engine on this computer"; the last answers stay "As of {time}"; Record this amendment is disabled; nothing is queued.
- **Stale / protocol changed**:
  - *The version changed after a count:* rows read "for the previous version" until counted again (10-query-workbench).
  - *The protocol changed since the page read it* (another tab, or an amendment by the assistant over MCP): the Plan's existing line "The protocol changed since this page was read." replaces the workbench's "In the protocol" group and the page re-reads; edits in the tab stay as "This version"; Record is disabled until the researcher has read the new protocol. What the engine refuses when the fingerprint sent is not current is Open question 6.
  - *After Record:* the page re-reads; new fingerprint and strings; the recall check reads "earlier protocol" (01's existing mark); the estimate card reads "The protocol changed since this was counted" and, when counted again, reuses the workbench's count log for the new version (a string already counted costs nothing); each earlier run reads `search_changed` as today.
  - *Open workspace proposals* become out of date; they are listed with their existing "Out of date" badge (01-proposals).
- **Accepted (read-only).** After the corpus is accepted, "Change the search" still opens the workbench; counts, seeds and comparison (with the corpus) work as above, and the version cannot be recorded. In place of "Record this amendment" the panel shows, as text and not as an error: "The corpus is accepted, so the search is not amended here. A new search added to this corpus comes with M3j; until then, start a new workspace from this protocol." (`plan.search.accepted`) with a link "Start a new workspace from this protocol" (the M10b Part A start-from-protocol path, `/[locale]/inception/import`). The version editing is still allowed in the tab (it can be compared) but "Use this version" is absent; Screening is untouched. No code is shown unless a stale tab meets `CORPUS_ALREADY_ACCEPTED`.
- **The amendment confirmation** (a state of this screen): opened by "Record this amendment" on a version that differs from the protocol. It holds, in order: the reason field; the **consequences list**; the counts before and after (from the log) or "not counted"; "Record this amendment" (brand) and "Cancel". The consequences, each only when it applies, in the engine's words keyed by `plan.search.conseq.*`:
  1. **Each earlier run, by id and size:** "run_002 (781 records) was searched with the earlier query. After this amendment it cannot become the corpus; harvest again." (`plan.search.conseq.run`). One line per run.
  2. **Staged exports, left untouched, with their stated strings:** "Scopus, 618 records, staged 20 Nov. Searched there with: {string}. It stays as it is. If you change that search too, export again and replace the file." (`plan.search.conseq.export`, `.exportAdvice`); with no string stated: "Searched there with a string you did not state." (`.exportNoString`). The browser lists them; it does not claim an export is wrong.
  3. **Recall checks and estimates:** "The recall check of {date} was made for the earlier query and no longer applies." and "The size estimate no longer applies; count again." (`.recall`, `.estimate`). Counts in the log stay and are reused.
  4. **Open assistant proposals:** "{n} open assistant proposals become out of date." (`.proposals`).
  The list is engine-written; the browser prints it and decides nothing. Harvest again is not offered here (it is Harvest's action); adding only the difference is M3j.
  After Record: "Amendment recorded. The search in the protocol has changed." (`plan.search.done`), with the backup folder under Details (`audit/amended/<stamp>/`) and the new fingerprint.

## Accessibility
All of `10-query-workbench` "Accessibility" applies (text and not colour; the counts table is a real table with caption and row and column headers; one polite live region for counting; hints at most 6 in the region; single keys only on list rows; RTL with `bdi` and `dir="ltr"` for numbers, strings, DOIs, run ids and fingerprints). Particular to Plan:
- The "Change the search" button has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` the region; the region is a labelled `section`.
- The confirmation is an inline region, not a modal, so the page stays operable; it is a `form` labelled "What this amendment changes"; the consequences are a `ul` of full sentences, never icons alone; a run's id and size, an export's name and its stated string are read as text. `role="alert"` only for a refusal; the "Amendment recorded" line is `role="status"`.
- "Record this amendment" is described (`aria-describedby`) by the consequences list, so a screen-reader user hears what it changes at the button. Its brand style is the only one on the screen; the disabled reasons are visible text beside it.
- The read-only text after acceptance is a paragraph, not an error; the link has a visible label.
- RTL: the two groups "In the protocol" and "This version" are labelled rows, not an arrow or a left-right diff; counts "before" and "after" are two labelled lines.

## Open questions
Questions for the researcher. The spec is `DRAFT` until they are answered. (Questions about the shared workbench, such as the merged concept's name, the databases counted, route names and the constants, are in `10-query-workbench` and are not repeated.)
1. **Where does the workbench open on Plan?** The route is fixed (`/discovery/plan`). Proposed: an in-page region above the two columns, closed by default. Alternative: a sub-route (`/discovery/plan/search`) with its own address, which keeps Plan's page unchanged and a reload on the workbench possible.
2. **How does the browser get the protocol's concepts?** `GET /discovery/plan` returns the query text, not the concept list. Proposed: the plan answer (or the workbench route) gains `concepts[]` and `golden_seeds[]` from `protocol.json`/`intent.json`. The milestone does not name the read.
3. **Who writes the consequences list, and does the corpus read return a staged export's stated string?** Proposed: the amend route's `dry_run` returns it, engine-written, from the runs, the staged exports, the recall checks and the open proposals; and `GET /screening/corpus` `sources` carries the string the researcher stated at staging (to be confirmed, the field was not read for this spec).
4. **The refusal for an empty or over-long reason, and its length limit.** Proposed: one sentence, up to the draft's `MAX_TEXT_CHARS`, an existing `BODY_INVALID` or a new code named at build.
5. **May an amendment be recorded for a version that was never counted?** The milestone says the journal row carries the counts before and after, and says `QUERY_NOT_COUNTED` is a note, never a blocker. Proposed: yes; the row says "not counted".
6. **What does the engine refuse when the protocol changed since the page read it** (another tab, or the assistant's amendment over MCP)? The milestone does not say. Proposed: the amend body carries the fingerprint the page read and the engine refuses with a new code or an existing one named at build.
7. **Do the neighbouring links now point to "Change the search"?** The estimate's "narrow" recommendation (`estimate.rec.narrow.go`), `RECALL_SEED_MISSED` and `HANDOFF_SEARCH_CHANGED` say "amend the query" and go to Inception today; the help hint `plan.amend.help` is no longer true for the query. 8. **No pool on Plan.** The workspace's `audit/recon_context.json` holds the first search's terms. Proposed: M11a does not read it, so Plan offers suggestions only from known papers and comparison sets. Alternative: read it for per-term counts.
9. **Are the two fixed checks shown on Plan?** The milestone places `QUERY_NOT_COUNTED` and `SEEDS_CANNOT_TEST` at the field and at Review (Inception). Whether `GET /discovery/checks` serves them for a workspace is not said. Proposed: not in M11a; the seed warning appears in the workbench's Known papers section only.
10. **Which run does "found in the harvest" use?** Proposed: the selected run (`?run=`, newest by default), stated by id on the report; an older run's report is read-only. The milestone says "the harvest" without naming the run.
