What a miss means

Missed by one database only: that database does not index the paper, or its string cannot express the query. Usually fine if another database finds it. Missed by every database: the query's own words do not reach the paper. Other papers worded the same way are probably missing too.

Why one seed was missed

Its title and abstract say "UAS" and "foliar disease". Neither is in the query.

'uas' had no anchor in the inception pool, so it was kept off the search string.
