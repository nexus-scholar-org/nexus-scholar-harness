# 09 Citation chase    Status: DRAFT | Phase 1 Discovery | Route `/[locale]/discovery/chase` (desktop only) | Milestone M3g-citation-chasing (engine, routes, screen)

Boards: none. No canvas board exists for this screen. The layout is built from the patterns of `Harvest` (D2, live legs table, one Cancel, stages) and `Handoff` (D8, the GatePanel and the Accepted card), as specified in `03-harvest.md` and `08-handoff.md`; no new visual language is introduced. Records: `docs/roadmap/milestones/M3g-citation-chasing.md` (DoD items 1-7 and decisions 1-4); plan `docs/roadmap/production-plan.md` §4 (mobile), §6 row "M3g-citation-chasing"; decision `docs/decisions.md` 2026-11-11 "Citation chasing comes after M4d, before the parked phases". Brand: `docs/ux/brand/BRAND_BOOK.md` (GatePanel: the dashed `brand` border is for the gate only), `docs/ux/brand/tokens.json`. Shared rules (step bar, `?console=`, job streams, refusal cards, hard rules): see 01-plan.md, "Shared by 01-08".

**Purpose.** After the screening is published, chase the citations of the included studies (forward and backward), read what is new, and send it to screening as a second, separately counted round (the "citation searching" arm of PRISMA 2020).

**Changed from the canvas.** There is no canvas; every point below is where the spec reuses or departs from the Harvest and Hand-off boards.
- **Legs are per seed x database x direction**, not per database. Harvest rows are databases; here a leg is one seed, one database and one direction (`legs[]`). The table shape is open question 3.
- **One Cancel for the whole chase**, as Harvest: "Stop the chase and keep what was fetched". A cancelled chase keeps what it fetched (the run folder stays, `CANCELLED` job) and publishes nothing, so no accept is offered for it. No per-leg stop, no retry button (the route has none); "Chase again" repeats every leg.
- **No running total while it runs.** As Harvest: only per-leg `fetched` from `leg` events. Counts appear after the run, from the run's `counts` (engine-written); the browser sums nothing.
- **Forward cap.** A leg with `cap_hit` reads "at least {fetched}" (role `unclear`), as in Harvest. Backward legs have no cap (`cap` is `null`; no "of" bar).
- **The gate.** The Hand-off's GatePanel is reused for "Send these {new} works to screening as round 2". Unlike the Hand-off, the gate states the archive consequence in full (round 1's decisions are kept, read-only).
- **Machine output as a draft.** The legs and counts are the engine's record, not a proposal (no draft styling). The seed list and the titles of chased records are data; nothing on this screen is an AI judgement and nothing is recorded as HUMAN except the accept itself (`CITATION_CHASE_ACCEPTED`).
- **Cut and not built**: multi-hop chasing in one run (decision 1: one level per run; a later round's includes are chased by running this screen again); per-seed cap field (the body has `cap_per_seed` for all seeds; shown as one field in Options); any "relevance" or "recommended seeds" claim (the browser makes no domain decision); export of the chase edges (`edges.json` is in the run folder, not on this screen).

## Entry / exit
- Entry: the discovery step bar gains "8 Chase" after "7 Hand-off", shown only when `screening.published` is true (open question 1); screening Home's published state offers "Chase citations" linking here; a direct URL. Before publication the step is absent and a direct URL shows the not-published state.
- Exit before the accept: "Go to the Publish screen" (phase 2, `/screening/publish`, in the not-published state), "Read the PRISMA report" (`/screening/...` report, secondary), "Back to discovery". After the accept: "Go to screening" (Home, where Prepare lives, `?console=` carried), exactly as the Hand-off does.
- Gate: **Send these {new} works to screening as round 2** = `POST /api/v1/discovery/chase/accept`. It is the only way a chased record enters screening. Nothing is sent to screening by the run itself.

## Data out
- Start: `POST /api/v1/discovery/chase/runs` ✗ M3g, body `{directions?, providers?, cap_per_seed?, ceiling?, seeds?, note?}`; only fields the researcher changed from `defaults` are sent. `202 {job_id}`. The job runs in the console process (like the harvest); journals `CITATION_CHASE`. Writes `literature/citation_chase/chase_NNN/` (`chase.json`, `raw.json`, `edges.json`, `new.json`). Allowlist entry (browser proxy `route.ts` / `browser.py`) is part of M3g.
- Cancel: `POST /api/v1/discovery/jobs/{id}/cancel` ✓ (`discovery.py:887`).
- Accept: `POST /api/v1/discovery/chase/accept` ✗ M3g, body `{chase?}` (the screen sends the selected run's `chase` explicitly). Answer `{round: 2, corpus {artifact_id, studies, records, accepted_at}, archived[], parent_corpus}`. It journals `CITATION_CHASE_ACCEPTED`, `SCREENING_ROUND_ARCHIVED` and `CORPUS_SNAPSHOT_ACCEPTED` (round 2). The page re-reads `GET /discovery/chase` after the answer and trusts the engine's `accepted`, never its own state.
- Nothing else. No prepare, no settings write, no decision write.

## States
- **Default (published, no run yet)**: eyebrow "Citation chase · round {round}"; h1 "Chase the citations of your included studies"; lede "The chase asks OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar which works cite your included studies and which works they cite. What is new is screened as a separate second round and counted in its own arm of the PRISMA flow." Card **Seeds**: source ("Included studies"), "{chaseable} of {total} can be chased"; the "not chaseable" list ("{title}: {reason}", for example no DOI, OpenAlex id or Semantic Scholar id), collapsed when long, never hidden. Card **Databases** with key state: OpenAlex (key set / not set), Semantic Scholar (key set, or "No key: slower, shared quota"; optional). Card **Options**: directions (both by default; forward only; backward only), databases, forward cap per seed (default `defaults.forward_cap`, help "Citing papers are capped per seed and database; a capped count is a lower bound. References are not capped."), run ceiling ("{run_ceiling} new records; beyond it the run is partial"), optionally the Unclear seeds option (open question 2), and "Note for this run (optional)". The one brand button "Chase citations" is a job start. Under it: "Keep this window's console open until the chase ends; closing the console stops it and keeps what was fetched." Start is disabled, with the reason in text, when `chaseable` is 0.
- **Not published** (`screening.published` false; refusal `SCREENING_NOT_PUBLISHED`, 409): the screen shows no form. Title "Publish the screening first." Body "Citation chasing starts from the studies you included, and those are final only once the screening is published." Affects: the chase. Action: "Go to the Publish screen" (`/screening/publish`, phase 2 screen 09). Not an error (role `exclude`).
- **Key missing** (`OPENALEX_KEY_NOT_SET`, 409; `keys.openalex_key_set` false before Start, or the refusal on Start): the card shows "The chase needs an OpenAlex key." and the exact line `SCHOLAR_OPENALEX_KEY=<your key>` with "Put this line in the console's environment, or in a .env file, then start the console again." and "Check again" (re-reads `GET /discovery/chase`), the same line-to-set pattern as the Plan screen (01). Start is disabled with the reason while `openalex_key_set` is false. Semantic Scholar without a key is a note, never a refusal.
- **Job active** (`DISCOVERY_JOB_ACTIVE`, 409, `details.job`; or `active_job` on read): "A {kind} is already running in this workspace." If the job is a chase, "Follow the running job" reattaches to its stream; otherwise a link to the screen that owns it (Harvest or Recall). Start is disabled while `active_job` is not `null`. `WORKSPACE_JOB_ACTIVE` (409) on the accept reads the same way ("Wait for the running job to end, then send these works").
- **Running**: eyebrow gains the chase number and the start time (UTC, from the job); badge "Running" (brand role, text). The legs table (one row per seed x database x direction as `leg` events arrive; the last event per leg wins): Seed (study title, `dir="auto"`), Database, Direction ("Citing it" for forward, "Cited by it" for backward), Fetched ("{fetched} of {cap}" for forward, "{fetched}" for backward; "at least {fetched}" and the lower-bound sentence when `cap_hit`), Status in words, Detail (`error_code` word or note). No total is shown while it runs. The side card **If you stop** has the one secondary button "Stop the chase and keep what was fetched"; its body: "Fetched records are kept in the run folder. Nothing is published and nothing is sent to screening. Earlier runs are not changed." The form is gone.
- **Finished** (job `succeeded`, run `SUCCESS` or `PARTIAL`, `published` true): h1 "The chase is finished. These works are new." The **Counts** card (engine numbers as written, each with words): "Fetched from the databases" (`raw`), "Found more than once in this chase" (`duplicates_within_chase`), "Already in your corpus" (`already_in_corpus`), "Published outside the year window {year_min} to {year_max}" (`outside_year_window`), "Beyond the run ceiling" (`over_ceiling`, row shown only when above 0), "New" (`new`). The **Legs** table (as Running, from the run detail). The **Not chaseable** list (seeds skipped with their reason). The **Already in your corpus** list (open question 4). Then the **gate panel** (below).
- **Gate panel** (finished, `new` > 0, not yet accepted; depends on decision 4 and the researcher's answer, open question 5). GatePanel pattern: `surface` with a dashed `brand` border, label "Gate · send these works to screening", the line "Send these {new} works to screening as round 2." and the consequence stated plainly: "Round 2 is screened separately from round 1. Your round 1 decisions are kept and archived read-only in literature/screening_rounds/round_001/; nothing is deleted. The new works become their own corpus snapshot that names the round 1 snapshot. The PRISMA report then shows round 1's published numbers in the databases arm and this chase in the citation searching arm." The one brand button "Send these {new} works to screening". A line: "The accept is recorded with the time and the snapshot fingerprint." Beside it only secondary links.
- **Accepted** (`accepted` not null on the selected run): no gate, no Chase again. h1 "These works are with screening as round {round}." Card **Round {round} corpus**: `accepted.artifact_id` (mono, selectable), `accepted_at`, studies and records as the accept answered (or the latest read), parent corpus id, and the archived paths (`archived[]`, only when the answer was just received; otherwise "Round 1 is kept in literature/screening_rounds/round_001/"). The one brand button "Go to screening". Starting another chase is only possible after round 2 is published (the engine says so with `SCREENING_NOT_PUBLISHED`; the screen shows the not-published state again, now for round {round}).
- **Empty**:
  - No chaseable seed (`chaseable` 0): "No included study can be chased." Affects: the chase. Body "A study needs a DOI, an OpenAlex id or a Semantic Scholar id; the list below says why each one cannot be chased." Start disabled; the not-chaseable list is shown. Not an error.
  - Nothing new (`EMPTY_RESULT`, `new` 0; refusal on accept `CHASE_NOTHING_NEW`, 409): "The chase found nothing new." The counts card still prints every row so the researcher can see why (everything already in the corpus, or outside the year window). No gate, no accept. Actions: "Chase again" (secondary) and "Back to discovery".
- **Loading** (skeleton, `aria-busy`): h1 area, seeds card, three leg rows; no number before `GET /discovery/chase` has answered; the Start and accept buttons are absent until then. While the stream connects to a known active job the table shows rows as they arrive; before the `snapshot` it shows "Reading the chase". During the accept the button reads "Sending the works to screening" and is disabled; there is no optimistic Accepted state.
- **Error** (typed refusal, `StateCard`; the code under Details, shared rules in 01-plan.md):
  - On the read: `PROTOCOL_MISSING` (409), `PROTOCOL_INVALID` (422): shared cards; `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`; `TOKEN_SCOPE_REFUSED`.
  - On Start: `SCREENING_NOT_PUBLISHED` (409: the not-published state); `OPENALEX_KEY_NOT_SET` (409: the key state with the exact line and "Check again"); `DISCOVERY_JOB_ACTIVE` (409); `PROVIDER_UNKNOWN` (422, "A database in the request is not known to the engine." the screen only sends the engine's `defaults.providers` values, so this should not occur; the code is shown under Details); `CHASE_DIRECTION_INVALID` (422, "Choose forward, backward or both."; `aria-invalid` on the directions group); `CHASE_SEEDS_INVALID` (422, "The seed choice is not valid."; `aria-invalid` on the seed option, or Details only if the option is not offered).
  - On the accept, shown above the gate with the page kept: `SCREENING_NOT_PUBLISHED` (409: not-published state); `CHASE_NOT_RUN` (409: "There is no published chase run to send." action "Chase citations"); `CHASE_NOTHING_NEW` (409: the nothing-new empty state); `CHASE_ALREADY_ACCEPTED` (409: re-read and show Accepted; it is the finished state, not a failure); `PROTOCOL_CHANGED_SINCE_PUBLISH` (409: "The protocol changed after the screening was published, so its includes no longer belong to the current protocol. Screening has to be redone and published under the current protocol before a chase can be sent." with the link to screening Home; no second accept; the engine offers no restore); `WORKSPACE_JOB_ACTIVE` (409: as above); `ARCHIVE_INCOMPLETE` (409: "Round 1 could not be archived completely, so nothing was changed." names the moved and not-moved paths from the engine under Details, with "Try again"); `PROTOCOL_INVALID` (422: shared card). Any other code: the engine's code under Details with its message and "Try again"; the page never guesses a meaning for an unregistered code.
  - A **failed job** (`failed` event, `job.state === "failed"`, `job.error.code`): every leg failed: "Every database failed, so nothing was fetched." (`PROVIDERS_FAILED`), with the failed legs listed by `error_code`; no `new.json`, no accept. An unclassified failure: the engine's message under Details.
  - `DISCOVERY_JOB_NOT_FOUND` (404): the shared sentence ("The console was restarted, so this job is no longer known...") with "See the chase runs".
- **Partial**: a run `PARTIAL`. (a) A failed leg: the table row is role `blocked` text ("Failed · {error_code in words}", text and icon, no fill behind text) with the engine's error text under Details; a summary line names the failed database and direction ("{provider} failed for {n} seeds" is shown only if the engine's legs say so; the browser lists the failed rows by name rather than counting) and says "Their works are not in this run." **The accept stays available** for the works that were fetched. (b) A `cap_hit` leg: "at least {fetched}", with the sentence "The database stopped at the cap, so this count is a lower bound." (c) The ceiling: a run with `over_ceiling` > 0 says "The run stopped at the ceiling of {run_ceiling} new records; {over_ceiling} more were not kept." (role `unclear`); the accept stays available. (d) A cancelled chase (`failed` event, `job.state === "cancelled"`): h1 "You stopped the chase. What was fetched is kept." and "Nothing was published and nothing is sent to screening."; no accept; "Chase again". (e) Skipped legs (`skipped`, for a seed the database cannot answer) read "Skipped" with the note.
- **Offline**: the engine is local; offline means unreachable. The last read stays with "As of {time}"; Start, Stop and the accept are disabled with "needs the engine on this computer"; nothing is queued. A dropped stream follows the shared rule (reconnect once; then "Connection lost · as of {time}", one `GET /discovery/jobs/{id}`, and Retry). If the connection fails during the accept, the page does not assume a result: "Try again" re-reads and shows what the engine says (it may answer `CHASE_ALREADY_ACCEPTED`).
- **Stale / protocol changed**: the read does not say whether the protocol changed. The page does not compare fingerprints; the real check is the engine's at the accept (`PROTOCOL_CHANGED_SINCE_PUBLISH`, above). An older run in the run list (not the newest) is read-only (no accept, no Chase again), with the sentence "A later chase replaced this run." A run whose `published` is false (cancelled, failed) never offers the accept. The year window printed (`year_min`, `year_max`) is the run's, as stored.
- **Live-only**: yes (no demonstration marker).

## Desktop layout
Source: no board; composed from `Harvest` (D2) and `Handoff` (D8) as specified in 03 and 08. Regions in order: shell and step bar ("8 Chase" active); eyebrow with the chase selector (run list, "chase_002 · started {date} · {status}", newest selected, older read-only), h1, lede, state badge; the **legs card** full width (running and finished); main: **Counts** card, **Not chaseable** card, **Already in your corpus** card (open question 4), **Notes** (`notes[]`, the run's note); side: **Seeds** and **Databases** cards and **Options** (ready state), **If you stop** (running), then the gate panel (finished) with the single brand button, replaced by the Round card and "Go to screening" after the accept. The gate panel uses the GatePanel pattern: `surface` with a dashed `brand` border, label "Gate · send these works to screening". The leg bar (forward legs only) is a decorative twin of the text numbers. Row roles: `include` = ok and not capped, `unclear` = capped, over the ceiling and partial notes, `blocked` = failed (text and icon only), `exclude` (neutral) = skipped, stopped, not chaseable, brand = fetching. One brand button per screen: "Chase citations" in the ready state, the accept in the finished state, "Go to screening" after it.

## Mobile layout (≤ 640 px)
Desktop-only (plan §4: write, gate and job screens are desktop only; the accept is a gate and the chase is a job). No phone board exists. ## Keyboard
Focus order: skip link, shell, step bar, chase selector, h1, seeds card (the not-chaseable disclosure), key state and "Check again", options (directions, databases, cap, ceiling, seeds option, note), then "Chase citations" or "Stop the chase"; the legs table (rows not focusable), counts, lists, secondary links, and the gate panel's accept **last in the page**, so a keyboard user reaches it after the evidence. No single-key shortcut accepts, starts or stops. `Enter` in a text or number field does not submit. While running, focus stays on the Stop button; when the run ends focus moves to the h1 and the result is announced (`aria-live="polite"`). After the accept, focus moves to the h1 "These works are with screening..." and the result is announced; after a refusal, focus moves to the card heading (or the field named by a 422).

## Open questions
Questions for the researcher; each has a recommendation. The spec stays DRAFT until they are answered.
1. **Entry point.** Add a step "8 Chase" to the discovery step bar (shown only once the screening is published), plus the "Chase citations" link on screening Home's published state, or offer the entry on screening Home only? *Recommendation:* both. The step bar keeps the chase findable from discovery and the engine already refuses it before publication; a Home-only entry hides a discovery job inside screening.
2. **Unclear seeds.** Is "Also chase the unclear studies" a checkbox on this screen, or a CLI-only flag (`--seeds included+unclear`) with the screen always using the included set? *Recommendation:* CLI-only for the first release. The decision "which unclear studies count as seeds" is a methodological one (the Unclear ones were not included) and the researcher who wants it can state it in the command and the run's note; the screen then only prints the seed source the engine used. If you want the checkbox, the copy keys `options.unclear` and `seeds.source.includedUnclear` are already listed.
3. **Legs table shape.** One row per seed x database x direction (long: with 40 seeds, two databases and two directions that is up to 160 rows), or a summary per database and direction with a per-seed disclosure? *Recommendation:* the summary (four rows at most, each with the fetched count taken from the engine's legs and a "Show the studies" disclosure listing the per-seed legs), because the researcher's first question is which database and direction failed or was capped. The summary's numbers would need either a per-group field from the engine or a browser sum of `fetched`; the hard rule forbids the browser sum, so this needs `legs_summary` in the run answer (an engine follow-up in M3g). If you prefer no new field, take the per-seed table.
4. **"Already in your corpus" list.** Show it (it names which included study cites or is cited by which corpus record, so you can see why a work was not counted as new), or keep only the count? *Recommendation:* show it collapsed under the counts card; it is the evidence behind a number the researcher may doubt and the data (`already_in_corpus[]`) is already in the answer.
