**Purpose.** Check, before the harvest, whether the frozen query reaches papers the researcher already knows are relevant, database by database, and read where it does not.

**Changed from the canvas.** Every cut, with the board element:
- **No "Fix it" card** ("Add 'UAS' as a synonym of unmanned aerial vehicle", "Add 'foliar disease' as a synonym of plant disease", "reruns this check"). The recall check is check-only (decision 3): the executed query is always the protocol's and the browser never rewrites it. A synonym is a protocol amendment through inception (decision 3; packet §4.2). The screen says so in one sentence and links to the inception drafts screen, as in 01 (open question 2 there).
- **No gate** ("Gate · recall", "Amend and check again", "Run anyway, 1 missed"). A miss blocks nothing (decision 3). The side card is a plain "Next" card with the link "Continue to Harvest", which reads the same whether the seeds were all found or not. A check is not required before a harvest.
- **"Why one seed was missed" card** ("Its title and abstract say 'UAS' and 'foliar disease'. Neither is in the query."). Cut: no route compares a seed's words with the query. The browser would have to read abstracts and decide which words matter, which is a domain decision. The screen shows the facts the check stores: per database found or not, `title_check`, and the seed's title. The board's "What a miss means" card stays as two lines of fixed help text (`discovery.recall.mean.*`), worded as general guidance, not as a finding about a seed.
- **Anchor seeds** ("Anchor" source badges, "Seeds are inception's anchor papers plus any you add"). **Offered since M4d (replaces the earlier "Anchor seeds deferred" cut).** The researcher keeps anchor papers on Direction (inception 03); the engine compiles them into `protocol.json` `search_strategy.golden_seeds` at genesis (✓ M4d). When no check exists yet, the seed form is **pre-filled** with those DOIs: each row has `kind` DOI, the DOI, `source: "anchor"` (shown "Anchor") and a Remove button; the researcher can remove any, and add their own (`source: "you"`, shown "You") before checking. Nothing is hidden: the form shows the protocol's seeds as they are, and the check is not run until the researcher presses the button. Anchor rows have no title until a check resolves them. With no `golden_seeds` the form opens with one empty row as before. When a check exists, the form is pre-filled from that check's seeds (unchanged). The browser reads the seeds from the protocol, not from the inception draft (the workspace does not store the draft id). A stored check that carries `source: "anchor"` seeds still shows them as "Anchor".
- **"Add a seed" accepts "DOI, arXiv id or PMID"**. The route accepts `kind: "doi" | "arxiv"` only. A PMID is not a seed kind; the screen offers a kind choice, DOI or arXiv id, and no PMID. The kind is chosen by the researcher, not guessed by the browser from the text; whether the text is well formed is the engine's answer (`SEEDS_INVALID`, message names the first bad seed).
- **Headline "The plan finds 7 of the 8 papers... One is missed by every database."** The numbers are `found` and `total` and the missed list is `missed_everywhere` from the last check; the sentence is built from them with named slots. "Missed by every database" is the engine's `missed_everywhere`, not a browser count. No sentence is shown about what the miss means for the review.
- **"+ 3 more seeds, all found by OpenAlex"** (a browser-made summary row). Cut: the table lists every seed (at most 25).
- **"Checked ... · OpenAlex and Crossref answered from cache (same strings, dry run at 13:58 UTC)"**. The check has no `cache` field. Shows only `checked_at` (UTC) and the `query_text` the check ran.
- **"Recorded with the run: the seed list ... and the plan fingerprint"** is kept as fact: the check stores its seeds, per-database results and `protocol_fingerprint` (`DISCOVERY_RECALL_CHECK`, packet §4.2), and the run that follows prints it (`recall_check` in `GET /discovery/runs/{run}`). The caption "Seeds must come from verified retrieval output, never a guessed DOI" is kept as help for the seed field; the engine enforces it through `DOI_TITLE_MISMATCH`.
- **Provider columns** are the databases the check reports in `per_provider` (dynamic), not the board's four fixed columns. A database that the check did not ask is not a column.
- **Eyebrow "Step 2 of 7 · Recall check · gate"** becomes "Step 2 of 7 · Recall check" (no gate). The phase ladder, "Not production" and any terminal disclosure are cut as in 01.

## Entry / exit
- Entry: the step bar; "Continue to Recall" on 01; a direct URL; a reload mid-check reattaches (see Shared, job streams, rule 4).
- Exit: "Continue to Harvest" (link, secondary) to 03; the step bar. After a check finishes the screen stays and shows the result.
- Gate: none for the check (a miss blocks nothing). The Check button is disabled until there is 1 to 25 seed rows each with an id (the engine checks the rest). After the corpus is accepted the form is removed and the last check stays as a record (shared hard rule).

## States
- **Default** (a check exists): eyebrow "Step 2 of 7 · Recall check"; h1 "The query finds {found} of the {total} papers you gave it" (`discovery.recall.title`; named slots); lede "Each seed is looked up and compared by title first. A paper no database returns is listed; nothing is blocked." The results table (Seed, Source, one column per database in `per_provider`), the databases-asked card, the form "Check again" pre-filled with the last check's seeds (editable), "Continue to Harvest". A row whose id is in `missed_everywhere` says "Not found by any database" in the `blocked` role as text and icon only (no fill behind text). A bar shows `found` of `total` beside the numbers.
- **Empty** (`RECALL_NOT_RUN`, 404): h1 "No recall check yet"; lede "Give the papers you already know are relevant. The check looks each one up and says which databases find it." The form is open: with the protocol's `golden_seeds` pre-filled as "Anchor" rows when there are any (a caption "These are the anchor papers you kept in inception. Remove any you do not want, or add your own."), else with one empty seed row. Not an error; the code is not shown. "Continue to Harvest" is still offered (a check is optional).
- **Loading** (skeleton, `aria-busy`): h1 area, table with three rows. No `found` or `total` shown until the answer arrives. **Checking** (job running): the form is disabled; a "Databases asked" list fills from `leg` events (database, fetched so far, status in words); the earlier table stays visible with the caption "This is the earlier check; the new one replaces it when it ends"; one secondary button "Cancel the check". When the stream ends `done` the screen re-reads `GET /discovery/recall`.
- **Error** (typed, `StateCard`; codes under Details):
  - `PROTOCOL_MISSING` (409), `PROTOCOL_INVALID` (422): on load via the plan read, or on Check; the card stands for the screen with the inception link.
  - `SEEDS_INVALID` (422): the engine's message names the first bad seed; this is the one code whose card body is the engine's sentence verbatim (LTR) above the standard sentence "A seed could not be used. Nothing was checked." Focus moves to the first seed field.
  - `MAILTO_NOT_SET` (409): the card prints `SCHOLAR_MAILTO=<your email>` in a code block and "Put this line in the console's environment, or in a .env file, then start the console again.", with Retry (re-sends the same Check). The plan card of 01 shows the same state before Check when `mailto_set` is false, and Check is then not disabled (the engine decides).
  - `DISCOVERY_JOB_ACTIVE` (409): "A harvest or a check is already running." with "Follow the running job" if it is a check, otherwise a link to Harvest.
  - `DISCOVERY_JOB_NOT_FOUND` (404): the shared sentence.
  - A failed job (`failed` event, `job.error.code`): the card names the code (`DISCOVERY_FAILED` or a provider code) with `error.message` under Details.
  - `PROVIDER_UNKNOWN` cannot occur: the screen sends no `providers`.
  - `ENGINE_UNREACHABLE`, `TOKEN_SCOPE_REFUSED`, guard codes: shared.
- **Partial**: a database with `status: "failed"` in `legs` shows "{database}: {error_code}" (`PROVIDER_RATE_LIMITED`, `PROVIDER_TIMEOUT`, `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`, `PROVIDER_REFUSED`, or no code) with `error` under Details; its column reads `failed` in `per_provider`, which the table shows as "Failed" with text, and says nothing about whether the seed would have been found. A seed with `title_check: "DOI_TITLE_MISMATCH"` shows the code and "This DOI resolves to a different title; the seed was not replaced by another record." A seed `unresolved` shows "Not resolved: the identifier did not match any record." `n/a` is "Not that kind of record there" (a journal DOI on arXiv). A leg with `cap_hit` shows its `reported_total` as "at least {n}".
- **Offline**: engine unreachable; the last check stays readable with "As of {time}"; Check and Cancel are disabled; nothing is queued. A stream that drops is handled by the shared reconnect rule (once, then "Connection lost" and Retry).
- **Protocol-changed**: the check's `protocol_fingerprint` differs from the plan's now. The table stays (it is a record) under the banner "This check was made for an earlier protocol; the query below is not the current one." with `query_text` shown, and "Check again" is the single brand button. The step bar mark reads "earlier protocol".
- **Accepted**: `corpus.state === "ACCEPTED"`: the form and Check are removed; the last check stays as a record.
- **Live-only**: yes.

## Keyboard
Focus order: skip link, shell, step bar, h1, results table (cells are not focusable; the table has a caption), the seed rows (kind, id, Remove), Add a seed, Check these seeds (or Cancel the check while running), Continue to Harvest. `Enter` in an id field adds no row and submits nothing; `Enter` on a button operates it. After Add a seed focus moves to the new row's id; after Remove to the next row or to Add a seed. After a refusal focus moves to the card heading (or to the first seed field for `SEEDS_INVALID`).
