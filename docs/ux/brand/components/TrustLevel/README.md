# TrustLevel

The per-claim trust verdict with its reasons and what would change it, so a level is never shown without its why.

## Use

- Four levels map onto the status roles: Strong on `include`, Adequate on `brand`, Weak on `unclear`, Blocked on `blocked`.
- Always three parts: the level badge, one line of reasons in `body`, one line of "changes if" in `caption`. A level with no reason is not allowed.
- The claim text comes first in `body-strong`; studies are counted, and a named study links to its record.

## Don't

- Don't show a level as a score or a percentage; the rules are categorical.
- Don't colour the claim text itself; only the badge carries the level.
