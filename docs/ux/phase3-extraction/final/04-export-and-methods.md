**Purpose.** Take the charting table out as a file, see the PRISMA full-text counts the researcher's own events give, and copy a methods paragraph whose numbers are all computed and which says no more than the events show.

**Changed from the canvas and from 01.**
- The paragraph is written by the engine from the events (`report.py` `methods()`), in English, as a draft. The researcher does not edit it here; it regenerates identically.
- "Not retrieved" reasons are counted apart from exclusion reasons (01 question 2, closed).
- Exports never block. They say how many cells are not charted and how many required cells are empty (record (h)).
- 01 said the Export menu offers CSV and the spreadsheet; Part C adds the report page where the counts and the paragraph are read, because 01 has no room for them.

## Open questions
Questions for the researcher. Each has a recommendation; none blocks the engine.
1. **Where do the counts and the paragraph live?** Recommendation: a page of its own, `/[locale]/charting/report`, "Export and methods" for charting, modelled on screening's export page and reached from a toolbar link beside Export and from screening's export page. Cost: a seventh route and one more link. Alternatives: (a) a panel on the charting page: no new route, but the table's toolbar and the form panel already use the right side and the counts table is wide; (b) a block on screening's own export page beside screening's paragraph: one export page, but a researcher who has not published screening never finds it and the two paragraphs would sit side by side with different protocols' figures after a re-inception.
2. **Does the spreadsheet carry a sixth sheet, "Methods"?** The build has it (the paragraph, the Notes and Not stated, so the file holds the text that goes with its numbers); the record's (h) lists five sheets. Recommendation: keep the sixth sheet. Cost: record (h) and 01's Copy section are changed to six sheets. Alternative: five sheets, and the paragraph only on screen and by Copy paragraph.
3. **Should the CSVs guard against spreadsheet formula injection?** A value that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@`, typed by the researcher or taken from a title, can run as a formula when the CSV is opened in a spreadsheet program. The spreadsheet writes all text as text already. Recommendation: keep the values as they are in the CSVs, as screening's CSV does, and write nothing extra (a prefix would change the researcher's own data and break a round-trip). Cost: a CSV opened directly in Excel may evaluate such a cell. Alternative: prefix such values with an apostrophe in every CSV, and say so in the help line.
4. **Desktop-only on the phone?** Recommendation: yes, one card "Export and methods are on your desktop.", as screening's export (plan §4); no file goes to a phone. Cost: none new. Alternative: allow the page read-only on a phone (counts and paragraph, no download).
