## Scope notes (from the specs)
- **Phone QR pairing is M2.** Spec 09 puts `/lan/session/*` and `/lan/pair` in
  M2; desktop approval in M1b-1 uses the terminal-only approval code (M1a
  `pairing/begin`, then `redeem`, then `publish`). The plan row's "publish with
  QR pairing" is corrected to match.
- **Accepting the corpus from the UI.** There is no `POST /corpus/accept`
  route (M0 shipped the CLI). The Prepare screen's snapshot-missing state
  shows the `nexus-scholar accept-corpus` command instead of a button; the
  route is deferred.
- **Routes:** the new screens live at `/screening/home`, `/screening/triage`, `/screening/publish` and `/screening/prepare`, beside the legacy `/screening` page (its demo record and its live batch view are pinned by existing suites). The legacy page is retired at M5-slim.
- **Prefetch:** records may be prefetched (spec 03, "next 5 records");
  proposals never are, because reading one records a reveal (M1b-0 review P3-2).

- `POST /corpus/accept` (spec 02 accept button): the CLI step is shown instead.
- Phone QR pairing and `/lan/*`: M2 (spec 09).
- **To M2, a torn event or reveal line** (M1b-0 review P3-3; researcher, 2026-11-05): an unreadable line in `events/*.jsonl` or `reveals.jsonl` still fails intake with a 500. It is handled with the phone's offline queue, which adds the next writer.
