# H-screening-drafts Agent drafts a decisive answer can decide; the method changed after genesis as an amendment; a safer run screen
Branch: `dev/h/screening-drafts` (from `origin/main` `b9c7dc0b`) · Lane: **RELEASE** (see "Lane") · Status: **READY FOR PR** (head `48d29df9` before this record update; review round 2 APPROVE; PR gate green on `48d29df9`; decisions 1 to 10 accepted by the researcher 2026-11-20) · Started: 2026-11-20

Found by the acceptance run of 2026-11-20 (a real scoping review through the installed wheel; record `docs/roadmap/acceptance-run-sensor-stress-2026-11-20.md`; corpus of 1,062 works):

- **The unclear pile.** Agent drafts on Haiku 5.5 (220, $0.05) and Opus 5.5 (200, $1.20) were derived Unclear for 85% and 83% of the records, because `derive_outcome` (`src/scholar_harness/screening/proposals.py`) returns Unclear as soon as any criterion is answered `not_reported` under the default policy `unclear`, before it looks at a decisive answer. Re-derived outside the app with a decisive-answer-first rule the same drafts gave Haiku 96 Unclear, 107 Exclude, 17 Include of 220 and Opus 102, 85, 13 of 200: the rule, not the model, made the pile (M9a decision item 3 asked to settle this rule first).
- **The silent radio.** The researcher wanted the draft beside the record; Prepare offered "Show beside the record", but the protocol written at genesis sets `blinding`, `PUT /settings` refuses a change then (`BLINDING_SET_BY_PROTOCOL`), and the screen never sent one: the click did nothing. The not-reported policy, also written at genesis, could not be changed at all.
- **The run screen.** The model went back to `claude-opus-5-5` on every reload and the confirmation named neither the model nor the estimate (one click cost $7.59 where $0.22 was meant); a Batch run showed "0 of N proposed" for about six minutes (two runs were cancelled as stuck); "No run yet" for about 40 seconds after "Start the run"; "No accepted corpus" before batches were prepared although the corpus was accepted.

Choices: `docs/decisions.md`, entry "2026-11-20 — H-screening-drafts" (items 1 to 10, accepted by the researcher 2026-11-20; item 2 changes the accepted 2026-11-08 decision "Method parameters move into the protocol").

## What changed

**Rule table** (`screening/proposals.py`). `NOT_REPORTED_POLICIES = ("unclear", "exclude", "decisive_first")`; under `decisive_first` the definite failures are checked first (as for every policy) and only then a gap gives Unclear. `outcome_in_force(proposal, criteria, not_reported=, protocol_fingerprint=)` re-derives a stored draft from its stored output and checks; a draft made under another protocol keeps its stored outcome. `submit_proposal` derives under the policy in force (protocol plus amendments) and stores it as `not_reported_policy`.

**Method amendments** (`screening/method.py`). `amend_method` appends one line to `audit/method_amendments.jsonl` (`amendment_id`, `field`, `from`, `to`, `protocol_fingerprint`, `amended_by`, `amended_at`, `note`) and one `PROTOCOL_METHOD_AMENDED` journal row (a failed journal write takes the line back out, as `submit_proposal` does). `effective_method(ws, until=)` applies the amendments made under the current protocol's fingerprint, oldest first, up to `until`; `EffectiveMethod.amendments` and `source_of(field)` say where a value comes from. Only `blinding` (when the protocol sets it) and `not_reported` are amendable.

**Console** (`console/api/screening_v1.py`, `refusals.py`, `browser.py`). `POST /method/amendments` (desktop; added to the packaged UI's POST allowlist and the development proxy's). `GET /settings`: `not_reported_source`, `method_amendments`. The draft view, the queue's agent-uncertainty order and the agreement use the outcome in force; the draft view adds `recorded`. `_state` answers `BATCHES_NOT_PREPARED` when the corpus is accepted but no batch exists. Setup `last_model`; run view `batch`, `chunk_size` (stored on new runs, read from the estimate for older ones). The screening API contract (`docs/architecture/screening-api-contract.md`) and its recorded fixtures (`tests/fixtures/screening_api/`, regenerated: two files gain the new fields) follow.

**Methods paragraph** (`screening/export.py`). The `decisive_first` sentence; values at publication (`effective_method(ws, until=published)`); one sentence per amendment made before publication, and one on how drafts are counted after a rule amendment; `figures.method_amendments`; a blinding amendment is a change in the blinding timeline.

**Agent** (`scholar_agent`). `prompt_gate.derive` and `report` derive under the protocol's policy, or `not_reported` when given; reports name the policy. `measure(..., task_version=, not_reported=)` and `prompt-gate measure --task-version --not-reported`. `RunSpec.task_version` (default empty) so the blind check and the report name the version. `tasks/screening/prompt-screen-v2.md` and `[versions."screen-v2"]` (`measured = false`) in `task.toml`; `load_task(version=None)` and `candidate_versions()`. `agent_runs.calibration_result` scores the outcome in force.

**UI** (`apps/research-ui`). Prepare: the blinding radio on a protocol-set workspace and a new not-reported radio group open a confirmation and send `POST /method/amendments` only after "Record the amendment"; the amendments in force are listed; the rule table's `decisive_first` wording. Agent proposals: model from `last_model` or `CHEAPEST_ANTHROPIC_MODEL`; the dialog names model, count and estimate (a dry run fetches its own); "Starting the run…"; the batch-service note with a ticking wait; cards for `BATCHES_NOT_PREPARED` and `METHOD_AMENDMENT_INVALID`, and `BLINDING_SET_BY_PROTOCOL` reworded. Triage: "First recorded as … under the earlier not-reported rule" when the outcome in force differs. en/fr/ar throughout.

**Specs and design docs.** Amendment notes in `docs/ux/phase2-screening/final/02-prepare.md`, `06-agent-proposals-run.md`, `10-export-and-methods.md`, and `docs/architecture/agent-system.md`.

## Follow-ups
- **Re-inception does not archive `audit/method_amendments.jsonl`** (`inception/emit.py` `ARCHIVED_ON_REPLACE`). Amendments become inert once the fingerprint changes, but would apply again if an identical protocol came back (review, follow-up).
- **The export binds amendments to the current `protocol.json` fingerprint**, not to the publication's `protocol_fingerprint`; they differ only when the protocol changed after publication, when the publication is stale anyway (review, follow-up).
- **If the run estimate fails, the confirmation stays at "Estimating…"** with Start disabled; Back closes it and the refusal card shows (review, follow-up).

- **Inception (M10f-3, Shape and Criteria):** genesis writes `method.blinding = reveal_after_decision` and `method.not_reported = unclear` without asking. The redesign should ask both where the criteria are accepted, with the three rules in the researcher's words and a sentence on what each does to the agent's drafts (Prepare's wording can be reused), so a new review starts with the rule the researcher chose instead of needing an amendment. Not built here, as instructed.
- **The 40 s before a run starts** is now shown as "Starting the run…", but its cause was not investigated (`start_run` builds the estimate and the driver synchronously over the whole scope; on 1,062 records that may be the time). A profile on the real workspace would say.
- **Concurrent amendments** are not locked: two amendments sent at the same instant could both read the same "from". The app has one researcher and one confirmation at a time; a lock like the event log's would close it.
- **`screen-v2`** waits for the researcher's approval of the measurement spend (decision item 10); making it the default is a separate decision after the measurement.
- **Measure the rule on the frozen set:** the M9a measurement could be repeated under `--not-reported decisive_first` to see the effect on recall (no such re-derivation exists for the 2026-11-19 reports: they keep outcomes, not answers).
