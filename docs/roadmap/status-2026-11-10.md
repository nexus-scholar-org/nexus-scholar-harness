## 3. What the review found (`docs/assessment/three-phase-hardening-2026-11-10.md`)

The three phases pass end to end over the API, through publish and export.
Sixteen findings, the ones that matter:

- P1: replacing a protocol keeps the old corpus and decisions in place, so
  the new protocol cannot search. Needs the researcher's call (back up
  `literature/`, or refuse).
- P1: PubMed `[ab]` is not a field tag; the term is silently widened.
- P1: OpenAlex requires an API key since February 2026; we still send the
  email parameter and call the key optional.
- P2: no "protocol changed" signal on the runs list, the hand-off, the
  screening summary or the queue; the PRISMA binding check is vacuous after
  an edit; the corpus view shows zeros for a discovery corpus; validation
  errors bypass the refusal envelope; inception drafts have no size limits.

The provider reference (`docs/kits/search/PROVIDERS_2026-11.md`) has the
per-provider facts and dated changes.
