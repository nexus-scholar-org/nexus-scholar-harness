# Brainstorming: Methodology & Research Design Tooling

This folder contains expanded specs and arguments for 5 tooling concepts aimed at solving methodological mismatch and poor reading strategies for early researchers.

1. `scholar-design-kit.md`: A CLI toolkit for planning, pre-registration, and power calculation.
2. `methodology-copilot.md`: An interactive conversational agent that grills researchers on their paradigm.
3. `three-pass-triage.md`: A workflow leveraging PDF extraction and LLMs to automate Keshav's Three-Pass approach.
4. `methodology-vector-space.md`: An extension to `scholar-rag-kit` for querying research designs instead of just facts.
5. `research-question-refiner.md`: An agent skill that refines vague questions into rigorous, paradigm-specific hypotheses.
