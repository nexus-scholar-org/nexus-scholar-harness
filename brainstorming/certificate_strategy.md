# Certificate Strategy: Verifiable Credential
**Decision:** Credly + LinkedIn Badge
**Status:** Designed ✅ | **Tier:** Paid (Capstone completion required)

---

## The Credential

**Badge Name:** Nexus Scholar — Certified Research Automation Practitioner

**Issuer:** Nexus Scholar Suite (self-issued via Credly)

**Trigger:** Capstone project passes evaluation (≥70%)

---

## Why Credly?
- Industry-standard platform for verifiable digital badges
- One-click "Add to LinkedIn" integration
- Badge has a **public verification URL** — any employer/university can verify it
- Free issuer tier available for small volume
- Integrates with LinkedIn's Skills & Certifications section

---

## Badge Metadata (Credly fields to configure)

| Field | Value |
|:---|:---|
| **Badge Name** | Nexus Scholar: Research Automation Practitioner |
| **Description** | Awarded for completing the Nexus Scholar Course and a peer-reviewed capstone project demonstrating automated literature research using the Nexus Scholar Suite (6 open-source Python tools). |
| **Skills Tagged** | Systematic Literature Review, Python, Research Methodology, BibTeX, RAG Systems, Citation Networks, Academic Writing, Research Automation |
| **Level** | Intermediate |
| **Time to Earn** | ~40 hours |
| **Expiry** | No expiry |
| **Evidence** | Capstone PDF + GitHub repo link |

---

## The LinkedIn Flow for Students
1. Complete all 26 lessons
2. Submit capstone project
3. Receive pass notification by email
4. Receive Credly badge email → Accept badge
5. Click "Add to LinkedIn Profile"
6. Badge appears under **Licenses & Certifications** with a verified link
