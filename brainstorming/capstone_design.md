# Capstone Project Design: "Build Your Own Nexus Workflow"
**Status:** Designed ✅ | **Tier:** Paid

---

## Philosophy
A traditional multiple-choice exam proves memorization. A capstone proves mastery. Instead of testing what students remember, we test what they can *build*. The capstone is the student's first independent research automation project, guided entirely by the skills they learned across the 26 lessons.

---

## The Core Challenge
> "Choose a real research problem from your own field. Design and implement a complete, automated literature research pipeline using the Nexus Scholar Suite. Document every decision you make."

---

## What the Student Must Produce (The Portfolio)

### Section 1: Research Question Design (Module 2 skills)
- A formally stated research question using the **PICO/SPIDER** framework
- Justification for the chosen methodology (quantitative/qualitative/mixed)

### Section 2: The Search Protocol (Module 2 skills)
- A documented `scholar-search-kit` query in at least **3 sources** (OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref)
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria table
- A PRISMA 2020 flow diagram showing filtering steps

### Section 3: The Analysis Pipeline (Module 3 skills)
- A `scholar-graph-kit` citation network visualization with at least 50 nodes
- A `scholar-rag-kit` session demonstrating 5 non-trivial research questions answered from their corpus
- A completed Synthesis Matrix (at least 10 papers, 4 themes)

### Section 4: The Write-Up (Module 4 skills)
- A 1,500–2,000 word structured mini-review (IMRaD format)
- A clean `.bib` file generated and repaired using `scholar-bib-kit`
- A bibliography formatted in **2 different citation styles** (demonstrating format-independence)

### Section 5: Workflow Reflection
- A written reflection (~500 words): What worked? What would you change? What did you automate that used to take you days?
- A flowchart of the student's final custom pipeline

---

## Submission Format
- A single PDF document containing all sections
- A GitHub repository containing all code, scripts, and data files
- Optional: A 5-minute screen recording walking through the pipeline

---

## Evaluation Rubric

| Criterion | Weight |
|:---|:---:|
| Research question is well-formed (PICO/SPIDER) | 10% |
| Search protocol is reproducible and documented | 20% |
| Graph & RAG analysis shows genuine intellectual insight | 25% |
| Mini-review is structured, coherent, and properly cited | 25% |
| Workflow reflection is honest and specific | 10% |
| Code/scripts are clean and runnable | 10% |

**Pass threshold:** 70%

## Certificate Trigger
Successfully submitting and passing the capstone is what **triggers the Credly badge issuance**. No capstone = no verified credential.
