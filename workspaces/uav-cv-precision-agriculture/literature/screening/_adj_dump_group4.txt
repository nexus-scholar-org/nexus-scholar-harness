VENUE: 2025 3rd International Conference on Sustainable Computing and Smart Systems (ICSCSS) | YEAR: 2025 | DOI: 10.1109/icscss64956.2025.11500716
ABS: Increasing need for sustainable agriculture yield, maintenance and food security requires innovative methods in weed management, particularly in labor-intensive paddy crops. This paper presents a novel and optimal framework for real-time detection and removal of weeds in paddy fields using Edge Artificial Intelligence (Edge AI) technologies. Conventional methods, mostly rely on manual labor to detect and remove the weeds. The proposed system integrates convolutional neural networks (CNNs) installed on edge devices to empower immediate decision-making in the arena. To distinguish between rice plants and common aggressive weeds such as barnyard grass and jungle rice the model is trained, even under challenging situations like similar morphology or even under variable climatic factors. Real-time processing is accomplished with lightweight AI models enhanced for edge positioning on devices like NVIDIA Jetson Nano, drones, robots and Raspberry Pi with AI accelerators. Field examinations prove that a significant reduction in herbicide use, improved detection accuracy (up to 92%), and greater operational efficiency when compared to traditional methods. The proposed study authorizes that Edge AI offers an environmentally friendly, scalable and economically feasible solution which yields more crops for precision agriculture in rice farming.

The optimized model is run on low‐power devices such as smartphones, Raspberry Pi, and farm drones without the need for cloud computing, allowing real‐time detection in far‐off fields. Field trials using drones validated rapid image capture and inference performance. This study delivers a scalable, cost‐effective, and accurate early pest and disease detection framework for sustainable agriculture and supporting food security at the global level. The model has been successfully implemented with TensorFlow Lite within Android applications and Raspberry Pi systems.

TITLE: Socially Informed Priority Scoring for Cost-Effective UAV Weed Spraying
VENUE: International Conference on Computing for Sustainable Global Development | YEAR: 2026 | DOI: 10.23919/indiacom70271.2026.11526640
ABS: The use of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in automated weed control is getting more widely used to reduce labour requirements and reduce unnecessary herbicides. The existing systems, however, only optimise on the detection accuracy and do not consider the socio-agronomic priorities on which the real spray decisions in farming practises are made. This study offers a socially informed, cost-conscious priority scoring system that helps the UAVs automatically decide when and where it is appropriate to spray weeds. The pipeline integrates: (i) SAM-ViT-H vegetation mask detection, (ii) species detection with a fine-tuned EfficientNet-B2 classifier, and (iii) an interpretable severity score based on patch size, weed density, and a species-based coefficient that indicates perceived aggressiveness. Experiments on the DeepWeeds dataset show strong operational performance, achieving 95.14% precision, 95.14% recall, >92% weed-coverage recall and only 0.61% false-positive spray activations. The framework improves decision accountability by combining data-driven severity analysis with crowdsourced preference learning of weed species and their aggressiveness. This enables UAVs to balance ecological risk and herbicide expenditure while maintaining human interpretability. By integrating social signals into autonomous spray prioritisation, this work bridges technological capability with user-driven sustainability agendas, advancing the broader adoption of intelligent and publicly aligned precision agriculture.

TITLE: Detection Method of Concrete Surface Cracks in Bridge Structures Using DeepLabV3+ Enhanced with Mona
VENUE: 2026 6th International Conference on Consumer Electronics and Computer Engineering (ICCECE) | YEAR: 2026 | DOI: 10.1109/iccece69169.2026.11399794
ABS: Bridge crack detection technology is gradually transitioning from manual surveying to automated detection using mobile platforms such as drones and unmanned boats. However, this new detection mode imposes high demands on algorithm real-time performance and faces the challenge of class imbalance in images. To address these issues, this paper designs a multi-strategy data preprocessing process and proposes the MDLab lightweight semantic segmentation model, which incorporates a Mona-Integrated Multi-scale Feature Module and a Multichannel Feature Fusion Module. With only 23.9M parameters, the model achieves a mean Intersection over Union (mIoU) of 88.27%, which is 1.58% higher than DeepLabV3+, and processes a single image in 22ms, a 45ms reduction compared to U-Net. The results demonstrate that the proposed model achieves high segmentation accuracy while maintaining a lightweight architecture compared to mainstream models.

ID: SCI-001224
TITLE: Crossseg: Cross-Scene Few-Shot Aerial Segmentation Using Probabilistic Prototypes
VENUE: IEEE International Geoscience and Remote Sensing Symposium | YEAR: 2023 | DOI: 10.1109/igarss52108.2023.10283144
ABS: In this work, we propose a novel framework called CrossSeg that addresses the task of few-shot semantic segmentation for different aerial imagery. Conventional semantic segmentation approaches struggle to generalize well to unseen object categories, making them a significant limitation for modern intelligent systems, especially those deployed in realistic real-time settings, such as unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). CrossSeg overcomes this limitation and generalizes well in a cross-scene setting with only a few labeled samples. Unlike traditional methods that use a set of fixed prototypes for each class, CrossSeg utilizes high-quality probabilistic prototypes that can not only represent different semantic classes but also handle significant variations in different scenes. Experiments show that our approach significantly improves upon conventional few-shot segmentation baselines and does not require extensive tuning.
