## 1. Adjudication Protocol & Decision Logic

During Title/Abstract screening, 74 records were identified that satisfy **INC-01** (UAV aerial imagery and agricultural crop context), but whose abstracts lack explicit quantitative benchmark metrics (e.g. numerical `mIoU`, `F1-score`, `FPS`, or `latency` in milliseconds).

### Adjudication Rule:
1. **Primary Screen Status**: Provisionally categorized under `EXC-06` (Incomplete Benchmark Reporting in Abstract) to ensure the initial `included.json` candidate set ($N=786$) consists strictly of confirmed empirical benchmarks.
2. **Traceability & Full-Text Contingency**: All 74 borderline records are preserved in `conflicts.json`. During full-text PDF retrieval (PRISMA Stage 3), if high-interest papers (e.g. *BAWSeg*, *Rice field weed sensing*, *Instance segmentation method for weed detection*) contain benchmark tables in their full text, they can be promoted to the quantitative extraction matrix.
