Dear Editor,

Please consider our systematic review for publication in **Computers and Electronics in Agriculture (Elsevier)**. We report the first *trust-audited* quantitative synthesis of deep-learning segmentation and on-device edge-inference evidence on agricultural UAV imagery.

**What the review contributes**

1. **A verified, audited corpus.** From 1,837 records identified across OpenAlex, Crossref, Semantic Scholar, PubMed, and arXiv (2018–2026, English), 138 full texts were retrieved and 94 studies with clean, trust-audited extractions entered the quantitative corpus. Every numeric claim was re-verified against the source full text via 354 claim-level checks, with three quote-backed corrections recorded and disclosed — not silently "cleaned."
2. **Comparability grounded in evidence, not assumption.** Because 83 of 88 datasets are used by a single study, cross-study aggregation is statistically invalid; we therefore organise RQ1 around paired *intra-study* architecture comparisons, which isolate model effects from dataset effects, and we quantify the dataset-reuse problem directly.
3. **A deployment evidence base for RQ2.** We define the embedded true-edge cohort (n=15) explicitly, separate it from desktop/cloud measurements, flag two widely cited "edge" papers that never measured on-device (with verbatim deferral quotes), and report precision, resolution, throughput, latency, power and energy reporting rates.
4. **A trust layer (RQ3).** Zero retractions or corrections among 94 studies, deterministic QUADAS-2-adapted risk-of-bias scoring (overall low 2 / unclear 77 / high 15), open-science artifact audit (public data links 21/94, code 18/94), and conflict-of-interest disclosure (11/94), with screening-reliability transparency (κ=0.115 disclosed, 690 disputes fully adjudicated).
